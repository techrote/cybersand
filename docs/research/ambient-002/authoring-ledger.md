exercised with isolated temporary fixtures: altered original inputs,

Those passes establish verifier behaviour only. They are not native gameplay,
graphical execution, actual Windows execution or physical owner acceptance.
Integrated Godot, native, graphical and extracted-Windows results must be
recorded against their final source identities.
