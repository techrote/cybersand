Status: **complete source-bound Linux programme independently verified**. The
final candidate supports physical goal changes, remembered-interest revisits,
obstruction withdrawal, social observation and finite exploration/home cycles
through the ordinary composable policy system. Its strongest measured gains are
reduced ground steering oscillation and recovery in the registered adverse
fixtures. The results do not establish universal exploration superiority,
reliable global navigation or owner acceptance of entertainment/playfeel.

## Exploration, steering and control policies

The main 30-second comparison uses three matched seeds for each supported rig.
The random control changes legal steering every 0.1 seconds; the stronger
`random_hold` control holds a seeded heading for one second while renewing legal
commands at the same cadence. Scripted control is an ordinary deterministic
physical policy. Ground coverage uses horizontal 16-cell bins, excluding vertical
gait from exploration credit. Flight coverage uses two-dimensional 16-cell bins.

## Memory and adverse physical outcomes

Memory's clearest measured benefit is recovery when a route or attraction is
unproductive. These are physical outcomes under matched initial conditions, not
synthetic agents:

| Adverse case, two seeds | Stateful | Memory disabled |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sealed attractive goal | 7 internal failure events; 6.2 seconds selected withdrawal; 40.58% active stall | 0 failure-memory events; no withdrawal; 80.27% active stall |
| Route blocked during the episode | 5 internal failures; 9.1 seconds withdrawal; 24.81% active stall | no withdrawal; 92.98% / 93.98% active stall |
| Material moved during the episode | no obstruction-failure events; 6.47% active stall | no obstruction-failure events; 8.82% active stall |

In the blocked-route case, stateful horizontal range is about 65.6 cells versus
33.2 without memory. The finite five-second outcome window finds three
eight-cell recoveries for the stateful actor and none for memory-off. This is
consistent with useful withdrawal, but retreat does not make an inaccessible
goal reachable. The sealed goal remains a real obstruction. Wall-proximity
entries, internal failure events and physical retreat measurements have separate
definitions and must not be interchanged.

The five-minute stateful wheel remains a negative result: only 51.74 cells of
horizontal range, four stable bins and 36.66% active stalls. Memory-off has 52.20
cells, four bins and 45.58% stalls. This is improved from the retained older
candidate's 68.3% stateful stall fraction, yet it is still a limited local
inhabitant, not a reliable long-range navigator.

The two-minute stateful flyer covers 184.37 horizontal cells and 37 stable flight
bins, selects `seek_home_drive` for 26.2 seconds and records two geometric
departure/return cycles. Memory-off covers 169.55 cells and 28 bins, with one
geometric cycle. A geometric return alone is not proof of homeward intent; the
selected module and drive/phase trace supply the additional mechanism evidence.
The ground scavenger has no home module in this programme, so its zero home
returns are not failed homeward attempts.

Intentional inactivity is separate from body stillness. For example, the final
stateful Runner main trials have a median 15.71 cells of accumulated body-center
path during neutral commands, including gait and momentum. This quantity must
not be presented as deliberate exploration or net displacement.

## Social activity and population limits

Social observations come from actual copied actor values and are checked against
the other physical traces. Four-actor courtyard grouping is stronger with the
social modules enabled: 94.67% / 94.42% grouped actor-time versus 44.33% / 44.17%
with the relevant social modules disabled. The trajectories show the latter
actors separating toward opposite sides of the World.

That evidence is scoped. Jitter-random actors remain grouped for 90.17% / 100%
of the same windows because they travel little. At eight actors, stateful
grouping is 85.63% / 69.88%, below the existing social-roamer comparison's
91.54% / 85.17%. With a peer legally moving away, stateful paired grouping is
24.00% / 18.67%, also below social-off's 34.33% / 32.33%. The system does not
universally hold groups together or recover departing leaders.

Grouping means membership in a connected component under a 40-cell pair-distance
threshold. Initial components are now reported separately. Subsequent formation
counts are exact-set/threshold changes, not evidence of enduring friendships,
intentional new communities or stable leadership. The eight-actor experiment
uses eight actual articulated actors; there is no 16/32-actor integration or
abstract-agent substitution.

Deterministic procedural seeds do not imply bit-identical physics. The retained
same-source 83a local-versus-hosted comparison had only **23,252 of 67,200
corresponding positions exactly equal**, 44 of 194 complete actor traces equal,
and a maximum position difference of 118.99 cells despite matching initial
material/pose/profile and engine/native identities. That remains evidence of
physical host/execution variability, not an identity mismatch to erase.

Actual graphical captures and the separate ordinary-Workbench sustained observer
address presentation. These scientific plots alone cannot decide whether the
inhabitants are entertaining to watch. The evidence supports richer reusable
physical behavior with visible limitations, and a playable evaluator for owner
review. It does not support autonomous global planning, guaranteed resting
stillness, universal social cohesion, blanket superiority over simple controls,
or promotion beyond Draft/HOLD.
