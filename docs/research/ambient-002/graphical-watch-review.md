# AMBIENT-002 — ordinary Workbench observation at a725

## Decision

The ordinary Workbench produces recognizable, physically embodied activities in these three observed scenes. Common Ground gives the clearest social vignette: an inhabitant approaches peers, watches them, regroups and then moves away before approaching again. Pipe Orchard makes persistence visible through repeated approaches to an inaccessible oil cabinet and finite withdrawals. Foundry Row shows an exploratory flyer returning to its authored home, entering a brief home-rest phase and resuming exploration. These are ordinary profile modules acting through the real actor owners and actuators.

The result remains qualified. The refinery sequence becomes repetitive and never demonstrates a solution to the blocked goal. The flyer exposes a concrete inconsistency: its home-rest arrival radius is 20 cells, while the drive-recharge proximity test uses 16 cells. It rests about 16.49 cells from home and its exploration drive continues to fall. This observation is retained as an **a725 negative baseline**, including every original frame and decision sample. A later correction, if qualified, must have a separate application identity and receipt; it cannot replace these observations.

This is an interesting configurable evaluator with visible temporary activities, rather than proof that every profile produces a convincing daily life. The hosted renderer is too slow to establish owner hardware playfeel. The screenshots and sampled movies make behavior inspectable, but do not provide continuous real-time footage.

## Common Ground: a visible social cycle

[Sampled movie](evidence/sustained-2eea3dbb/ambient2-courtyard/media/ambient2-courtyard-sampled-timelapse.mp4) · [Contact sheet](evidence/sustained-2eea3dbb/ambient2-courtyard/media/ambient2-courtyard-contact-sheet.png) · [Original decision report, lossless gzip](evidence/sustained-2eea3dbb/ambient2-courtyard/frames/watch-report.json.gz)

The social group is visible on the shared promenade. At the approximately three-second sample, Social Drifter #1 is watching a copied peer with neutral intent. It then regroups. At seven seconds it has selected `disperse` at x = 266.90; at ten seconds it is at x = 206.66 and still dispersing. At eleven seconds its intent changes back to observing/approaching a peer and it moves right again. Later samples show another withdrawal from x = 342.15 at 42 seconds to x = 311.15 at 44 seconds, followed by another approach. This is a change of place around other inhabitants, not merely gait movement.

Across samples from requested seconds 3–60, the followed Runner spans 145.82 horizontal cells. Its sampled path is 487.54 cells but its net displacement is only 48.64 cells. That distinction matters: the activity is repeated local social movement, not exploration of the whole settlement. Its bounded memory ends with eight visited cells and 19 recorded reentries. The combination of approach, watch, regroup and dispersal is recognizable in the frames and copied decisions. It does not establish conversation, cooperation, a shared plan or stable social roles.

This is the strongest first scene for an owner to inspect. The amber group remains near the same portion of the promenade while individual distances change. The other tagged group provides a second local cluster, but this observation has no affinity ablation and therefore does not isolate the causal effect of tags. The separate matched social experiments remain the appropriate evidence for that comparison.

## Pipe Orchard: persistence with an unresolved goal

[Sampled movie](evidence/sustained-2eea3dbb/ambient2-refinery/media/ambient2-refinery-sampled-timelapse.mp4) · [Contact sheet](evidence/sustained-2eea3dbb/ambient2-refinery/media/ambient2-refinery-contact-sheet.png) · [Original decision report, lossless gzip](evidence/sustained-2eea3dbb/ambient2-refinery/frames/watch-report.json.gz)

Persistent Scavenger #5 repeatedly approaches the sealed cabinet. It is near x = 945.10 at three seconds and x = 945.03 at nine seconds. A withdrawal then moves it to x = 926.82 at ten seconds and x = 902.43 at twelve seconds. It resumes investigation and subsequently revisits a remembered material observation. Similar finite withdrawals recur later. At the final sample it is again revisiting an interest, with 14 failure events recorded in its bounded obstruction memory.

The brief inspection phase around seconds 37–39 is outside the cabinet, near another accessible oil patch. It is not evidence that the sealed target became reachable. Across requested seconds 3–60, #5 covers only 45.25 horizontal cells, despite a 420.34-cell sampled path. Its final net displacement is 31.11 cells. The actor therefore presents a legible stubborn personality and genuinely leaves the wall during recovery, but remains caught in a local return-and-withdraw loop.

This is useful adversarial evidence and an initially interesting vignette. Watching the same cabinet for a full minute becomes repetitive. More successful long-range exploration or reliable abandonment of persistently inaccessible goals has not been established by this scene. The nearby Routine Patrol is dominated by Water avoidance in every sampled intention after three seconds, which is another clear limitation of the authored placement/profile combination. Its name alone does not demonstrate a completed patrol.

## Foundry Row: authored home arrival succeeds, recharge does not

[Sampled movie](evidence/sustained-2eea3dbb/ambient2-settlement/media/ambient2-settlement-sampled-timelapse.mp4) · [Contact sheet](evidence/sustained-2eea3dbb/ambient2-settlement/media/ambient2-settlement-contact-sheet.png) · [Original decision report, lossless gzip](evidence/sustained-2eea3dbb/ambient2-settlement/frames/watch-report.json.gz)

The followed Cautious Flyer moves beneath the suspended awnings, with 115.42 cells of horizontal range and 38.74 cells of vertical range after the initial three seconds. The sequence is visually different from the ground group. Its memory records 13 visited cells by the end. The Boundary Grazer elsewhere in the same World moves much farther, but most of its sampled intentions have no selected temporary goal; its movement should not be described as successful material-boundary following without the corresponding observation evidence.

The Cautious Flyer's copied intent switches to `seek_home_drive / approach` by the 44-second sample. Its authored home center is (365, 355). Distance falls from 68.14 cells at 44 seconds to 56.69, 43.98, 31.28 and then 18.37 cells at successive samples through 48 seconds. It enters `home_rest` at 48 seconds and remains in that phase through 51 seconds, with neutral intent. It coasts about 1.89 cells in the first resting interval and then covers less than 0.005 cells of sampled path from 49 to 52 seconds. Exploration resumes at 52 seconds. These observations support an actual physical return and temporary home-rest phase; neutral intent alone was not used as proof of stillness.

The same record exposes the defect. At 48 seconds exploration drive is 0.313915; at 51 seconds it is 0.298981. After coasting, the flyer is approximately 16.49 cells from home, inside this profile's authored 20-cell arrival radius but outside the existing 16-cell recharge test. It therefore starts home rest without qualifying for home recharge. The completed physical return is positive evidence; **a replenished exploration drive is not**. The original record remains intact for any later corrected-source comparison.

## Presentation, performance and interpretation limits

The native side elevations are coherent and editable: the common promenade, cabinet, material basins, awnings and overhead facades remain visually distinct. Overview is useful for understanding each layout, but actors are small compared with the architecture. Follow selected makes their movement easier to see and can be combined with another zoom step. In close Follow view, the flyer has a notably quieter, more isolated presentation than the ground population.

The copied overlay is informative. It shows the current target, visited cells, authored home/interest regions, nearby peers and navigation rays without creating another perception path. It is also visually dense. Peer/model labels overlap, and long reason text can be clipped near the edge of the visible area. The default actor labels identify model and policy, not personality. A viewer needs Inspect to connect a particular body with its named profile. C inspected all three contact sheets and representative full-resolution raw frames; all 183 images were hash-verified, but this review does not claim continuous playback or a human timing assessment of every rendered frame.

These timings include the observer's PNG encoding and copied diagnostic collection. They are not a no-observer benchmark, monitor latency or physical NVIDIA acceptance. The movies' accelerated playback must not conceal this slowdown.

The position summaries use approximately one sample per simulation second and begin at requested second 3 to reduce initial falling/settling. They can miss motion between samples, include gait, and undercount travelled path. Sampled phase frequencies are not exact intention durations. Bounded recent-decision tails can reveal additional transitions but are not a complete 10 Hz history. These three observational scenes do not establish matched causal personality effects or superiority over held-random steering; the separately registered experiments address those questions.

## Suggested owner itinerary

Open the ordinary Workbench, choose **Actor Composition**, load **Common Ground**, select instance #1, press **Play** and choose **Follow selected**. Observe approach and separation before enabling the copied overlay; then use Inspect to relate the visible body to its Social Drifter profile and recent decisions.

Load **Pipe Orchard**, select #5 and watch the sealed cabinet. The useful question is whether its finite withdrawal changes its physical location, followed by whether it resolves or repeatedly returns to the same inaccessible interest. This recording shows the latter limitation.

Load **Foundry Row**, select #7 and follow the Cautious Flyer. Its return-home phase is legible near the end of the recorded minute. On the a725 source reviewed here, the home-rest/recharge discrepancy remains present. Any later repaired evaluator must be identified separately in the final decision packet. Profile changes remain staged through the normal editor's Apply/Revert workflow; this particular observation did not perform live authoring edits.
