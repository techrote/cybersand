## Conditional recommendations

1. **Retain production C64-E8.** The present evidence supports continued reversible
   contracts and experiments. It does not establish a production winner or require
   a storage migration before the separately scoped #200 foundation.
2. **Keep optional state explicit and bounded.** The new neutral-tail experiment
   demonstrates reservation, rollback, ownership and a spatial allocation cliff:
   dispersed use can allocate every chunk even at the same sparse nonzero count.
   Choose actual feature granularity from its measured density/lifetime; do not
   extrapolate this toy array result to whole-game memory or speed.
3. **Retain P2 compact/DUAL64 as experimental options only.** Consume the existing
   independent synthesis and its ABI/workload limits. Padding, logical capacity,
   array bytes and process RSS are distinct. No additional material/solver state
   becomes authoritative merely because a carrier can store it.
4. **Version semantic continuation independently of carrier width.** Preserve
   validated material/state/temperature, current-epoch touch at capture, absolute
   clock, activity/deadlines, ordered work, edit intent and baseline identities.
   Unsupported actor/Rapier/feature domains must pin/refuse. CYSD1 and raw structs
   do not provide complete continuation.
5. **Keep epoch, mutation generation, residency incarnation and job identity
   separate.** The native counterexample shows why content and completed tick
   cannot recover support after owner edits. The contract model's full-resident
   clear premise also prevents it from admitting a lazy-clear shortcut.
6. **Leave production and successor decisions separate.** New author evidence
   does not reopen #213, admit #212 or a pressure/inertial port, freeze schema/ABI,
   change defaults, add Rapier forces or implement GEN-006 streaming.
