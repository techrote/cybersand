# Experimental granular budgets and contact rest

## What is authorized and what remains a control?

The [registration](../operations/gcr-rest-registration.md) fixes budget5/10/20/40/80
and separate rest variants at20 before candidate acquisition. The inherited20
is a numerical response ceiling, not a physically demonstrated universal value.
LA and all earlier evidence remain controls. Ordinary default stays legacy/B0;
sleep, material policy and production/default promotion remain excluded.

The prototype retains actual520-member building mass, COM and inertia by default.
Explicit developer mass/inertia multipliers reach64; friction remains0..4 and
gravity0..368.48. These overrides do not rewrite native payload provenance or
qualify original mass. The4096 effective mass-normalization ceiling stays separate
and must be displayed/receipted when actual mass exceeds it.

## Who owns the response and its history?

Native owns current material, geometry/contact admission, normal/friction/torque
budgets and constrained rest proposals. Main-thread Rapier owns rigid-body state
and the one actual wrench application. Versioned immutable rest values travel
with the intended sample through the existing exclusive worker and snapshot.
No worker calls live Godot/Rapier APIs, no body becomes native authoritative cells,
and persistent contact memory never replaces a fresh support query.

Proposed rest context binds version, session, sample, actual gravity, known drive,
hard clearance and last applied sample. Unknown context cannot grant catch.
Rest proposals use an explicit versioned receipt; generic diagnostic dictionaries
do not authorize motion. Malformed/stale/failure/quarantine paths retain current
refusal, installation acknowledgement, support release and whole-reset semantics.

## How do the compared mechanisms differ?

P reuses only validated quiet contact impulses within the same current friction
cones and budget. It does not increase authored mu or store blocked controller
impulses. Its history/cache has fixed bounds, resets on incompatible support or
events, and advances only on distinct compatible applied samples.

R measures current overlap before suppressing unnecessary upward withdrawal;
positive or unknown overlap retains bounded positional correction. It also
separately tests omitting opposing-velocity zeroing before the precomputed wrench.
Position translation, velocity zeroing and impulse energy need separate ledgers.

C tests a native contact-constrained residual arrest, including angular motion.
The same contacts must also certify gravity load and torque equilibrium. A single
point or unsupported pivot may be singular and must refuse whole-body catch.
Current cones, node/global capacity, finite state, passivity, no unresolved overlap,
no drive/event and hard clearance remain required. Main requires fresh age0 and
unchanged sampled velocity. No global velocity assignment, pose latch or gravity
bypass is introduced. PRC combines only these preregistered mechanisms.

Low jerk or small COM speed alone never establishes rest. All contact-point motion
includes rotation. Post-step velocity zero can still drift during the next gravity
integration; NO_ADMISSION and NO_REST are valid reported outcomes. A later change
to response timing needs separate registration rather than a hidden extra impulse.

## What counts as evidence?

Use source-matched native/sanitizer and actual Windows/Linux host controls before
physical acquisition. Keep the88-case bounded primary population, window reuse,
negative controls, genuine hard-clear support loss, measured removed energy and
actual defaults distinct from exploratory overrides. Conditional excavation that
never acquires valid rest is NO_VALID_REST, not successful support-release proof.
Preserve incomplete overlap as null, hard-contact confounds and failed gates.
Separate sampled passive energy from correction/gravity/event/hard-contact work.
No owner acceptance, issue completion or merge follows from implementing the modes.

## What is implemented on this experimental branch?

**Current opt-in prototype, 2026-11-02:** native source181a8257 implements the
registered budget and P/R/C/PRC modes. This status describes source availability;
physical qualification and production acceptance remain separate. The original
LA/default paths do not enable rest history. `GCR-REST` requires the explicit
`GCR-REST-001-2026-11-02` fresh-session token. The comparison panels stage changes
until reset, display actual mass/inertia and effective scale, and keep control/20
as their initial experimental selection.

Native revalidates admitted source predicates/signatures and current endpoint
overlap before publishing any rest result, including cached getters. Observed
material mismatch or changed hard-surface revision retires rest authority until
fresh preparation. A byte-identical erase/restore entirely between queries is
not parcel-history tracking. Rest refusal leaves the prepared baseline response
available; it does not rerun relocation. Current overlap also controls R.

The main-thread bridge binds version, mode, budget, session and sample before
application. Catch additionally requires age0, unchanged sampled velocity and
zero correction in every mode. It applies one accumulated wrench and measures
pre-correction, post-zeroing and post-wrench velocities, angular velocities and
kinetic energies. Correction potential, old-zeroing removal, constrained-catch
counterfactual removal and measured impulse delivery remain separate quantities.
Native context, results and receipts cross the exclusive worker as copied values.

## What did qualification establish?

The [dated qualification](../audits/2026-11-02-gcr-rest-contact-comparison.md) records
all88 captures at594a1a73. R/PRC fails the synchronous entry/overlap gates; its
two constituent changes remain combined. No physical quiet admission, warm start
or catch occurred, so P/C and support release from admitted rest are unqualified.
Inactive P/C synchronous outcomes match control; asynchronous compound delivery
varies strongly and cannot establish their causal effect. Budget40 improves the
mass2 barrel,80 adds no benefit there, and partial-support/async results do not
calibrate a universal ceiling. Retain20 as the experimental control. No sleep,
default promotion or owner acceptance follows; PR351 stays draft and unmerged.
