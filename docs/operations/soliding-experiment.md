# Bounded reversible soliding experiment

## Registration before Stage A implementation

The integration inputs are remote main `3b1747a493d6ffb01661dcf4111f1b32ae89dbac`
and accepted #11 `d5f0de687283ec366ed4ff33160a274e2a35ddc6`. Integration is
validated before candidate implementation. #11 supplies only the documented
ordinary rectangle/load prerequisite. #19 is H-ready; H has not occurred.

**Experimental scope:** serialized native-owner observation of one explicitly
registered patch, at most 32 by 32 cells, with a two-cell support/reaction halo.
No world-wide discovery pass, ownership change or automatic powder cohesion.
Fixed scratch and component capacities must refuse incomplete candidates.
Proposed limits: 16 components, 256 members/component, 1024 members/patch,
64 rectangle shapes/component. Caller-selected patches are not production
world discovery. Incremental work uses existing activity metadata and bounded
patch rebuilds; a conservative mutation witness must catch change-and-restore,
not just final content equality or the wrapping per-cell update epoch.

All nonempty materials are diagnostic controls. Initially only authored Wall
and inert construction blocks may be considered cohesive; loose powder,
including Stone's granular/braced variants, and all liquids are ineligible.
A material's hard-contact property alone is not cohesion evidence. The initial
allowlist is experimental, not a newly inferred structural law.

Register 30/120/300 consecutive healthy included ticks (0.5/2/5 seconds at60Hz),
occupancy0.8/0.9/0.98 and minimum area16/64/256 as observer-only screens.
Evaluate all27 comparisons from the same immutable measured candidate records;
no27 solver reruns are needed. Five translations include negative coordinates
and32/64/128 boundaries, with native workers1/4 and1800 ticks. Additional
short transition fixtures exercise faults and exact thresholds. No epoch policy
changes; repeated epoch wraps must not manufacture rest.

Cases: solid4x4/8x8/16x16 islands; dense supported and loose unsupported Sand;
ring with a hole; diagonal-only contacts; narrow neck; different-material seam;
excavation and support removal; chemistry/state/temperature mutation; sleep/wake;
exclusion/re-entry; patch-edge truncation; component/membership/shape saturation.
Measure face membership, AABB occupancy, downward exposed support using existing
hard/#10 predicates, active/changed evidence, continuous mutation age, topology
changes, candidate births/invalidations, scan cells, component/member/shape
high-water and rebuild latency. A candidate touching the discovery boundary
is incomplete and cannot be admitted. Exclusion, observation gaps, failure,
mutation, body occupancy or lost support reset earned rest. Rest does not
certify arbitrary load paths. Track observation neutrality against fresh controls.

Gate A-to-B: at least one positive authored island, zero eligible loose-powder
controls, no invented membership across holes/diagonals/seams, explicit saturation
and prompt invalidation. Reject or narrow the stage if these conditions fail.

## Conditional stationary proxy scope

Cells remain the only material owner. Exact row/merged-rectangle geometry may
replace existing hard terrain only in an isolated diagnostic fixture; no second
collider or granular support force may be added. Preserve holes and meaningful
seams, use identity/revision, and invalidate before the next admitted Rapier step
on edits, relevant mutation or support loss. Shapes describe existing hard cells;
they do not make the narrow neck structurally sound. Dynamics need a separate
reviewed ownership ADR and explicit Astra admission after this evidence.

## Performance registration

Record work bounds, scratch/queue/member high-water, observer-off neutrality,
rebuild versus idle costs and p50/p95/max. Timings from correctness runs are
observations only. If an uncontended timing run is available, use7 interleaved
baseline/observer process pairs and120 warmup ticks; retain every pair/outlier.
Flag paired p95 regression above15 percent for review. No production performance
claim, PMU claim or exact replay claim follows from this screen.

and real compatibility/threaded Web paths apply to any changed architecture.
