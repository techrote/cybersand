# RAMCACHE and Strata accepted-feature integration

## Scope and owner decision

On 28 November 2026 the owner authorized integration, corrective reconciliation,
qualification, guarded runtime publication when required, mainline merges and one
portable Windows Workbench evaluator for RAMCACHE #745 and Strata Theatre 003
#785, including the complete intended Strata 002 predecessor. This authority is
specific to **RAMCACHE-STRATA-INTEGRATION-001**. The principal owns final merges;
three specialists independently cover durability, optical reconciliation and
qualification/package verification.

The owner physically evaluated the original RAMCACHE application on Windows 11
with NVIDIA TITAN RTX and accepted persistent-area travel, authored and evolved
state after complete application reopening, optional deferred HOT travel and
hover preparation. Approximately 200 ms HOT travel between Industrial Fire and
Foundry Gallery and approximately 3.2 seconds into Waterworks are **owner
observations of the original evaluator**, not measurements of the integrated
application. The remaining Waterworks catch-up delay is accepted. No replacement
simulation scheduler or speculative preparation optimization belongs to this
integration.

The owner also physically accepted the original Strata optical results for
optional use. **Classic remains the default.** The owner explicitly waived the
90% Classic-performance-retention admission requirement for optional enhanced
profiles. Original measurements and failed numeric flags remain unchanged:
Architectural 78.26%, Cinematic Deep Focus 80.59%, Gameplay Clarity 75.68% and
Diorama High 73.49% in the original extracted Windows cohort. This decision does
not waive correctness, native authority, durability, ordinary Classic regression
or visual coherence requirements, and does not turn a failed target into PASS.

The artistic preference **Diorama with Rear architecture optical depth 1.3** is
reproducible through the Optics Lab. It is an optional authored choice, not a new
global default. Authored optics, temporary inspection controls and application
quality remain separate. The feature-specific guides own the exact controls and
save semantics.

coverage receipts for 480 Linux and 457 Windows cases. Its eight-job scoped

graphical Windows/Linux and an extracted portable package. Strata's original

## Preserved limitations and follow-up

Preparation currently budgets HOT destinations at two native ticks or 5 ms per
pass, and WARM/COLD destinations at 32 ticks or 100 ms. Automatic prediction
consideration is approximately once per second. These conservative budgets and
the accepted Waterworks delay are retained as future performance-development
inputs. Native ticks remain indivisible and existing debt still needs payment.

Deferred travel can precede disk publication, but only the existing matching
durable acknowledgement establishes saved state. Safe exit and recovery retain
their explicit refusal paths. Unsupported actors, equipment, solver domains and
cross-area flux remain refused by the current persistent-area contract.

Enhanced optical profiles retain their measured cost and the original admission
and physical-interval failures. Classic remains available without allocating
enhanced-optics materials until a processed view is requested. Bright-daylight
readability, rotated-camera presentation and general persistent layered worlds
are not newly claimed by this integration.

Optical actor-reveal assistance retains its bounded eight rectangles and scan
of the first 64 published parts. Accepted main can admit higher actor/body
capacities, but those extra actors do not gain a new clarity-assistance claim;
their ordinary physical rendering and simulation remain independent.
