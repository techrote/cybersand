## How is the witness selected?

1. Retain the original exterior grain, source-owner tie and deduplication. Its
   outward normal is the negative of its existing geometric projection's inward
   normal. Reject zero/nonfinite normals and unsafe int64 coordinate endpoints.
2. Normalize that normal by its largest absolute component. For `k=1`, then2,
   round each scaled component to the nearest integer with exact half ties away
   from zero. This is a **Chebyshev/L∞ bound2**, not a cardinal distance bound;
   a diagonal witness can be `2√2` cells away, with Manhattan distance4.
3. Choose the **first geometrically eligible** witness: all12 cells of the
   centered/downward stencil union (`x±1`, `y−1..y+2`) must be free of every
   transient body ID. Do not use material packing to choose a deeper witness.
4. For that single selected witness, require the original source and every
   closed-square supercover cell of the straight center-to-center segment to
   be unmasked, actual capable stable powder. Include corner-only touches.
   No void, liquid, hard, Plant, moving-grain or foreign/own-mask shortcut passes.
5. Invoke the unchanged side9/9 plus downward8/9 predicate **once** at the witness.
   Any source/path/packing failure refuses; it does not retry deeper.
6. Apply the unchanged response at the **original** geometric source projection.
   Witness/path cells contribute no extra source, force, moment or capacity.

The preimplementation cardinal-ray proposal failed an analytic upper45°
half-plane example because the downward stencil still touched the body at
depth2. The geometric ray was chosen before any successor efficacy results.
It admits the named mirrored45° fixtures but can still conservatively refuse
shallow upward/stair/corner configurations within the fixed bound. Do not widen
the bound after results or claim universal orientation coverage.

Per side source, the helper has at most24 stencil-mask reads plus seven path
mask reads, seven path capability/stability checks, one shared predicate call
with at most19 stability checks, and34 material loads: at most65 material/mask
probes. Relative read footprint is `x±3`, `y−3..y+4`. Original raster, source
ownership and deduplication work are counted separately. Fixed stack values and
scratch suffice; there is no allocation, mutation, persistent support or cache.

Local packing is not world-floor connectivity or proof of confinement pressure.
Existing tick0 authoring and native sleeping/exclusion rules remain authoritative.
An admitted upright side with pure vertical slip still has zero velocity-created
normal load and therefore zero tangent response. Fixing admission does not
silently implement the owner's separate sidewall-load/friction suggestion.

## Which gates precede physical acquisition?

An independent bounded segment/closed-square clipping oracle must agree exactly
with the implemented integer supercover and geometry decisions. The registration
defines half-plane source/mask coordinates, signed seams, expected witness depths,
rounding ties, all eight inner and16 outer Chebyshev offsets, and these controls:

- All nine capable materials and Stone states0/1; Plant/Water/hard/void sources,
  moved/transformed cells, excluded active regions and fresh re-entry.
- All axes and45° quadrants/mirrors, including the upper45° counterexample;
  depth1 diagonal has four supercover cells and depth2 diagonal seven.
- Own/foreign masks in source, path and full witness footprint; corner-touch
  blockers/voids; first-mask-free-but-unpacked refusal; depth2 exhaustion.
- Films/loose grains under their actual packing/epoch premises; excavation,
  reset and fresh re-bearing; failed World and invalid normal/coordinate values.
- Query leaves full native material/state/temperature/hash unchanged. Actual
  adapter admits a clean side with zero interior overlap, retains original
  projection/bin/count and never turns witness depth into extra capacity.
- Pure vertical slip on an upright admitted side retains zero normal/tangent
  impulse without a source of normal load. Existing R2 mechanics/oracle pass.

Every named positive/negative and probe bound must pass **before** physical
efficacy capture. Correct implementation/oracle errors before that gate while
retaining failed trials; do not fit thresholds to dynamics. Native state tests
are not automatically full adapter reaction/payload qualification.
