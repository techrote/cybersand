### What this establishes

1. **The dominant gas-off cost is outside pressure preparation.** Even eliminating
   all current head work would remove only about 13–16% of these native ticks.
   Stress leaves ~34.4 ms/tick in the material/kernel path after preparation.
2. **The current cache has almost no head locality under active large scenes.**
   Cached/dormant touches ~86% of my-canvas columns, ~96% of bench-canvas and
   **100% of stress-canvas**. Stress cached/dormant falls back completely every
   measured tick (311.1 pressure invalidation rectangles/tick).
3. **This explains #472's poor leverage.** More expensive dependency discovery
   was aimed at a head phase that is only ~6.8 ms of a ~41.2 ms stress tick, and
   current activity already dirties every stress column.
4. **Gas topology is a distinct secondary bottleneck where it is admitted.**
   Enabling gas adds ~2.84 ms to my-canvas and ~4.45 ms to bench-canvas; the gas
   share is ~28–32% of total tick time. Stress needs a physical-capacity decision
   before meaningful gas-on timing, not an optimization-budget increase.
5. **Six-worker parallel execution matters substantially.** Cached_dormant gas-off
   improves from 10.00→5.98 ms my-canvas, 21.79→11.33 ms bench-canvas and
   90.54→41.21 ms stress-canvas. All six workers were observed in those paths.

## Variant and face-release discrimination

Gas-off reference/cached/cached_dormant six-worker totals are close:

| Scene | Reference ms | Cached ms | Cached dormant ms |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| my-canvas | 6.125 | 6.054 | 5.981 |
| bench-canvas | 11.993 | 11.151 | 11.335 |
| stress-canvas | 41.616 | 41.249 | 41.212 |

Dormant certificates save little Water motion in the active scenes: my-canvas
drops from ~26,980 to ~26,801 Water motion calls/tick; bench is effectively
unchanged and stress remains ~130,044/tick. This is active-work evidence, not a
negative claim about dormant benefit on settled fixtures.

The native face-release context adds a separately measured ballistic pass:

| Scene | Base tick ms | Face tick ms | Ballistic pass ms |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| my-canvas | 5.981 | 6.148 | 0.163 |
| bench-canvas | 11.335 | 11.647 | 0.429 |
| stress-canvas | 41.212 | 42.625 | **1.410** |

Face release is measurable at stress scale but is not the dominant baseline
cost. It also increases pressure invalidations (stress ~311→404/tick). A complete
impact-spray/Rapier measurement remains a Godot application task.

## Decision

**Next optimisation target: material/kernel neighbour-read and scheduling cost,
not another PressureFlow head-cache redesign.** This result independently aligns
with the earlier stress profile in `2026-11-06-stress-canvas.md`, where coordinate,
chunk and neighbour reads dominated sampled native CPU. PR #471's same-chunk
neighbour cursor is therefore the immediate candidate to execute and measure.

Secondary follow-ups, in order:

1. qualify #471 exactness and same-host A/B performance;
2. if #471 is useful, profile remaining material-kernel hot functions after it;
3. separately investigate gas component scaling/capacity before optimizing gas-on
   stress;
4. only revisit pressure-head invalidation if a cheaper producer-side narrowing
   can be demonstrated without whole-column discovery;
5. retain face-release cost as an additive stress budget and measure real Rapier
   impact spray separately.

No #472 implementation is imported. No production source is modified by this
decision.
