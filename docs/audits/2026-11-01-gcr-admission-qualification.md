# GCR admission-only successor qualification

## Disposition and authorized scope

**Mechanical admission: PASS. Integrated physical candidate: NO-GO.**
GCR-A1 fixes the clean side/up contact defect with a bounded material-interior
witness. Native query/oracle tests and actual adapter tests pass. With the
unchanged R2 friction, normal-load and event-budget laws, however, required
Sand/Dust/Rust bank gates and four impact-preservation cases fail. Timing triggers
investigation. Do not promote this candidate or infer that better contact
admission has qualified its response law.

law changes. Controllers, friction/load equations, coefficients, event-first
accounting, capacities, ejection/search, inertia/COM, ABI, delivery fences and
sleep remain unchanged. Ordinary B0 remains default; Y1 and retained R2 remain
explicit references. No persistent support, rest cache or body-to-cell conversion
was introduced. The category covers all currently capable granular solids,
not just Sand. REM-004/#83 remains open.

PR#346 remains frozen, draft and unmerged. The focused successor is a separate
stacked draft against that branch; it must also remain unmerged.
**AWAITING OWNER GAMEPLAY ACCEPTANCE** records an unfulfilled boundary, not a
request to accept this technically failed candidate. Previous Y1 preference is
not A1 acceptance.
