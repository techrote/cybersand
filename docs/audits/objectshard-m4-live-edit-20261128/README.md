## Design choices and preserved contracts

- One native edit transaction accepts at most 4,096 exact targets for one
  structural owner, or one separately fenced ordinary terrain batch. Deduplication,
  deterministic selection and exact geometry preflight precede mutation.
- A changed structural owner rebuilds connectivity/mass/geometry once per batch.
  Fragment transfer retains the established child reservation and ownership
  acknowledgements. Geometry capacity refuses before material debit; unexpected
  post-write failure quarantines and reports uncertain completion.
- Ordinary Eraser and Paint right-drag follow visible material through terrain,
  collapse and sleeping fragments. Foreground owned Cargo/Product also blocks
  hidden terrain edits. Cross-owner strokes are sequential and non-atomic.
- Precision cut remains one cell. Eraser and ignition use adjustable swept
  circles; Drill is a single circular placement; Slice is a previewed release
  commit with a bounded safe-admission deadline. Weathering is real native
  removal with recorded density/seed/pass and optional material filtering.
- A freehand release cancels all unadmitted work. Tool/camera/scene changes,
  resize, pause transitions and explicit cancellation retire old gestures.
  Already admitted work remains accountable until its receipt/publication.
- The observer records failed attempts even with performance recording off.
  Applied native work and displayed coherent publication remain separate states.
  Its 256-event/32-pending bounds exclude large target/publication payloads.
- Changed geometry preserves surviving material-point velocity through the COM
  adjustment and wakes sleeping material. It does not claim closed-system
  momentum conservation for intentionally removed material.
- Impact is deferred to protect mandatory qualification. No repair, welding,
  general body addition, stress/fatigue or M5 scheduling architecture is added.

The canonical [implementation contract](../../research/objectshard-m4-workbench.md#bounded-native-structural-transactions)
owns detailed interfaces, capacities and failure semantics. The
[owner guide](../../operations/objectshard-workbench-playtest.md) owns controls,
