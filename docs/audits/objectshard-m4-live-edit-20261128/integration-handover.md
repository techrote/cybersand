# ObjectShard live-edit integration and M5 handover

live-edit candidate. The final delivery record owns its qualification,

## What material batching changes

The ordinary eraser now follows visible material from terrain supports through
collapse into detached and sleeping fragments. Precision cutting, adjustable
structural erasing, deliberate Slice and Drill placement, seeded Weathering and
Area ignition share the same existing native ownership boundary. Material
removal preflights exact geometry, accounts intentional debit and reconstructs
the affected parent's connectivity/mass/shape once per admitted batch.

This is material-edit batching. The broader roster-wide physical motion lease,
pose acknowledgement and semantic completion architecture is retained. The
predecessor M5 handover's serial Windows coordination costs, sleeping-body
optimization and sparse-storage traversal questions are not declared solved by
a faster removal transaction. Owned-body count must remain separate from
simultaneously awake count and renderer callback cadence from native progression.

## Contracts to preserve

- Validate every copied witness before deduplication or stochastic selection.
  Foreign owner, World/session/transport, body generation, material, pose and
  geometry changes cannot become silent fresh-target mutation.
- Resolve the visible foreground. Uncuttable Cargo/Product still occludes hidden
  terrain; a real Empty hole may expose a lower owner. Cross-owner gestures are
  deterministic sequential work, not an atomic multi-owner transaction.
- Reserve exact cover/storage capacity before debit. A defensive unexpected
  commit failure reports the accounted changed prefix and holds the owner;
  no rollback is promised. Complete erasure retires the real body through the
  existing acknowledgement protocol.
- Install collider, mass, centre of mass and inertia before further motion.
  Retain material-point velocity through COM shifts and wake changed sleeping
  geometry. Open-system removal is not a closed-system momentum-conservation claim.
- Admit input between physical intervals. All routes allow one outstanding
  edit; continued stroke service is throttled near 30/s, while fresh presses and
  explicit Slice commits can request immediate service. The frozen source
  comment's unconditional rate wording must not be mistaken for the actual guard.
- Release cancels unadmitted freehand work. Slice release commits one immutable
  bounded line with a finite admission deadline. Escape, camera navigation,
  physical Window resize, tool changes, pause/resume and scene replacement
  retire previous intent. Already admitted work remains accountable.
- An authenticated Applied receipt, geometry installation and coherent Main
  presentation are separate stages. Tests must use a durable action-bound
  completion witness rather than requiring a transient global-idle phase that
  can disappear between callbacks. Empty Draining carriers require actual
  Empty-slot and retired-body checks, not an ownership-flag shortcut.
- Diagnostic history stays bounded, owner/session-specific and informative for
  refused or never-admitted input. Authored starts reconstruct recipes; they do
  not save live poses, detached damage, inventories or combustion checkpoints.

## Next evaluation and future work

Use the twelve-step owner route in the packaged Workbench guide, including
sustained editing after settling, camera changes and recording off/on. The
owner's Windows 11/Ryzen 5600X/TITAN RTX observations must identify this exact
archive and renderer. Preserve unsuccessful input attempts as well as the
native/renderer measurements; the new bounded edit history is designed for that.

After physical owner evaluation, first reconcile M4 with accepted mainline and
repeat the complete guarded delivery path. Broader sleeping-body scheduling,
occupied-domain caching, physical acknowledgement batching and alternative
small-debris representations belong to separately justified M5 work. Native
geometry remains linear in retained carrier area; low material count alone
does not prove low traversal cost. Measure before increasing budgets.

Impact, repair, detached-body addition, welding, stress/fatigue, buoyancy and
live-state persistence remain separate architectural work. The present tools
provide actual material removal and existing combustion; no cosmetic damage
substitute or independent physics mutation path is introduced.
