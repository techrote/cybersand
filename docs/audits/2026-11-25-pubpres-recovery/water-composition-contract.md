# Water composition contract — registered before execution

The original submerged actor failure remains valid. Stored Water can legitimately
remain inside a native body footprint when bounded ejection has no Empty target.
The repair must render that physical state explicitly without deleting Water,
replacing its solver, inventing an empty region, or using an old pose as current.

## Candidate

`CYBERSAND_DIAG_WATER_COMPOSITION=1` is default off and only applies to coherent
Workbench presentation. It selects only the current genuinely completed Rapier
pose with matching body/World/session/epoch/generation ownership. Two distinct
native receipts may prove its dependency: the exact native input actually
consumed by that completed step, or a native closure sampled at that same current
physical step and identical pose. A sampled closure must never be reported as
an input consumed by that already completed step.

The existing continuous swept geometry proof remains authoritative for adjacent
visible submissions, with its unchanged time/cell/shift bounds. The composed
proof permits only Empty and Water in both actually uploaded immutable material
histories; all other materials and unknown history refuse. A discontinuous
endpoint reentry requires initial admission or an actual preceding withheld
submission. The selected pose never advances past a recovery ceiling.

A separate actor draw layer composites the admitted Water actors over the actual
material framebuffer. It uses the same immutable current/previous textures and
frozen blend as the material presentation. At Water pixels, actor opacity is0.65;
at Empty it is1.0; the same material blend combines these coverages. Fractional
actor edges falling into any visible non-Water occupied cell are discarded,
including hard floor pixels whose centers were outside the native footprint.
Thus the shader does not conceal hard material under an opaque actor. All native
bytes, Water quantities, body masks, physics and material textures remain intact.

## Required evidence before selection

- Pure geometry and identity adversaries: solid/Sand/other material substitution,
  missing or wrong old image, old World/owner/sample/step, forged consumed status,
  out-of-bounds geometry, exhausted budgets and fractional hard borders.
- Actual mounted native/Rapier receipt and current pose checks, including original
  settled three-Needle reproduction, dry/wet movement and pause/Step/restore.
- First-read framebuffer tests of both histories, dry-to-wet and wet-to-dry blend,
  exact clipped fractional floor border, and unchanged native/render material.
- Actual submission transaction must retire skipped candidates without advancing
  history or camera mapping; a source change requires resulting-source tests.
- Ordinary mixed actors and moving camera/editing, sustained qualified capture,
  and separate physical Windows11/NVIDIA owner acceptance.

No result is admitted here in advance. Failure retains the original control and
keeps the candidate off. Raw results, source/runtime identities and independent
review are recorded separately from this prospective contract.

## Recovery amendment: retained proof and independent physical/image authorities

Registered before testing the amended implementation, 2026-11-25. The initial
composition implementation's positive headless checks do not resolve its actual
paused-camera graphical failure or its observed frequent omitted body-frames.
Those original results remain retained. This amendment changes the mechanism,
not the native, material, framebuffer, freshness or physical-progression gates.

A short native wrench ring can evict an already verified sample after later
legitimate body publications. Ordinary acknowledged Pause does not itself
publish samples: it returns before that seam. The original paused-camera
failure therefore does not establish ring eviction as its cause. Retain that
negative result and test explicit publication stress separately from elapsed
paused frames.
That retires its eligibility to apply forces; it does not invalidate the exact
initialized or completed pose already proved against the native closure. Retain
at most one immutable proof per physical member, keyed by the exact owner,
World, native revision/tick/sample, member generation and geometry, physical
step/completion clock and pose. A real step or owner retirement invalidates it.
Return a separate mutable copy to the resolver for this draw's coverage result.
Never cache Water/Empty geometry clearance or update an old completion clock.
Transport-only re-publication and changing blend weights do not manufacture a
new native dependency and must not cause otherwise valid paused poses to vanish.

Two additional authorities are subject to independent review and execution:

- `initialized_authoring_closure`: an actual initialized, accepted actor owner
  at session step zero and actual manual Rapier count zero, with no claimed
  physical completion. The current RID cache, exact admitted native sample,
  actor generation and uploaded native frame must describe the same pose.
  Existing material/terrain/owner readiness and both image-history guards apply.
  This is distinct from a consumed dependency or a completed physical pose.
- `current_completed_native_dependency`: the current real completed Rapier pose
  may be proved by its own exact, actually applied native dependency even when
  uploaded material is older. Preserve the consumed native World/tick/sample,
  input/output poses and completion clocks, separately from the image's native
  provenance. Require the same actual World/member and current completed pose.
  Never claim that an older image packet was consumed by that newer step.

Both image histories are still independently immutable uploaded resources. The
entire relevant continuous pose sweep must admit only Empty/Water; any visible
other-material cell or unknown history still refuses the Water composition.
The shader clips visible hard-material borders, including fractional pixels
outside cell-centre masks. An older image cannot confer physical authority.
An actual physical dependency cannot waive image coverage or presentation age.

Required new witnesses are initial paused full submersion; long paused stability;
separate actual publication stress past both sample-ring capacities; material resync;
actual newer completed poses with older visible Water; stale/replaced owner and
World refusal; current/previous Sand and solid-border refusal; repeated layer
disable/re-enable first reads; and actual submitted mapping under camera changes.
The layer retains at most one child and material, unbinds textures while disabled,
and must leave no stale framebuffer pixels. Source and engine/app transaction
identities accompany every result. No implementation in this amendment is
selected on the strength of the earlier headless or shader-only checks.

The native mounted fixture then found that the initial accepted prepared owner
has a real admitted sample, but no Main-side sample record: adoption deliberately
retires its provisional force ring and acknowledged Pause never publishes a new
sample. The repair captures that exact already accepted sample immediately before
adoption retires the ring. It records only presentation provenance, without a new
native publication, waiting target, body mutation or physical completion. The
initial closure still independently requires step zero, clock zero and identical
actual initialized pose. This seam must execute in the native graphical fixture.

## Prospective exact immutable coverage reuse

The source-bound native graphical v2-03 test demonstrated repeated budget
omissions for stationary submerged actors: the initial paused Needle had 54
omissions across 562 observed frames, including 38 shared-budget refusals; the
completed paused Needle had 73 omissions, including 55 shared-budget refusals.
The rewet framebuffer failure was an actual refused draw with 886 scanned cells
and 10 budget vetoes. These original results remain failed. They do not justify
increasing the scan deadline or accepting unproved coverage.

This amendment replaces the earlier blanket prohibition on coverage caching
with one narrowly specified immutable memo. Register it before executing the
new implementation: retain at most one **fully safe, completed** Water/Empty
sweep result per actual physical member. Its exact key includes owner/bridge,
session/epoch, member/generation/RID, World, actual uploaded revision and serial,
current and relevant previous immutable content identities/shapes/texture RIDs,
unknown-history state, both sweep endpoints and every rectangle. An inactive
previous image is excluded explicitly; activating it requires a matching new
proof. Unknown required image history cannot match a memo.

Reuse may avoid a repeated scan after this frame's shared deadline, because the
exact immutable geometry question has already been completely answered. It may
not create a new native dependency, pose, completion, actual submission or scan
count. The current physical authority is checked independently on every use.
Refused/partial scans never seed a memo. Any changed input requires a genuine
new scan with all original budgets. The pixel shader continues to clip other
materials from both actual uploaded histories. Memo entries are cleared at
owner retirement and remain bounded by admitted members; discarded candidates
may retain only this pure computation result, never visible-history credit.

Required adversaries cover changed current/previous content and activation,
unknown history, World/revision/upload, owner/member/shape, start/end pose,
Sand under either visible history, rejected scans, actual material mutations,
paused/camera stability after initial full proof, and zero fabricated physical
or submitted-history credit. Actual native framebuffer tests must demonstrate
that budget-driven repeated stationary omissions are removed. No performance
or final playability acceptance follows from the memo's unit tests alone.
