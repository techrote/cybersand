## Selected mechanism and the missing native authority

The single proposed mechanism is a **fixed-orientation, bounded translation
constraint along a uniquely selected straight clearance corridor**. It moves
the aggregate only; it never moves native Sand. The full six-rectangle union,
520 native tuples, both voids, mass, centre of mass and inertia are immutable.
The new mechanism is separate from disabled response and the current velocity
candidate, and remains disabled by default.

Current `cybersand.sol281.sand-contact.v1` is insufficient authority for a
position correction. It exports supported exterior witnesses and aggregate
overlap totals, not all interior cell locations or unoccupied/obstructed swept
space. Its `complete` flag still allows non-Sand and hot overlap to contribute
metrics. An interior normal or totals alone cannot authorize an escape route.

First qualify the proposed geometry on the existing complete frozen native
fields, including the original nine-cell interior, stationary and rotated
cases. A frozen-field certificate is test-only evidence. If any original
eligible recovery case admits a safe route, the implementation must add the
smallest bounded read-only owner certificate and main-thread application seam,

not reuse a historical frozen field as live authority. If the original cases
admit no route in this registered family, record a decisive bounded NO-GO with
executable counterexamples and preserve zero candidate RID mutation.

Arbitrary upward impulses, using a single cell's MTV as a compound normal,
filled AABB/hull collision, per-cell bodies, deleting Sand, wider displacement
search, angular resets, friction/cohesion and global solvers are rejected for
this experiment. They either lack a witnessed path or change a separate owner.

## Geometry, uniqueness and fixed capacities

All distances below are **new proposed mechanism bounds**, not historical
acceptance constants. Geometry uses the real oriented rectangle against the
complete closed native unit square. AABBs are traversal bounds only. Exterior
contacts, interior overlaps, sibling seams and both holes remain distinct.

| Item | Preregistered contract |
|---|---|
| Direction family | Both signs of world X/Y and the common body X/Y axes; at most eight directions. Collinear same-direction axes are deduplicated within `1e-10` Euclidean distance. No diagonal sweep is inferred from end poses. |
| Angle | Exactly zero candidate angular correction. Ordinary rotation is separate; a live certificate must be revalidated at the actual current orientation. |
| Per-step distance | At most `0.125` cells at 60 Hz, half the existing `0.25`-cell witness drift bound. This is not a larger source-pose tolerance. |
| Lifetime distance | At most `4.0` cells, at most 32 full steps and at most 32 applied corrections per token/generation/epoch. Four cells bounds this experiment near the preceding observed depth envelope; local MTV depth is not presumed to equal compound escape distance. |
| End separation | Require positive `2e-5`-cell separation from every nonempty unit square, checked with exact oriented geometry; this uses the retained depth precision rather than enlarging occupied shapes. |
| Swept path | Compute the continuous interval of positive overlap for every rectangle/cell pair from all four SAT axes along the whole proposed line segment. Endpoints alone cannot authorize a path. No previously disjoint pair may acquire positive overlap anywhere in the sweep, including a sibling taking over an originally overlapping cell. |
| Existing Sand | Ambient eligible Sand is the only initially intersecting material that may be reduced. Initial and every subsequent full swept path must satisfy the original initial-depth-plus-`0.25` limit continuously. Area/MTV and pair identities are recorded independently. |
| Hard/excluded states | Any positive initial overlap above the native `1e-9`-area filter with hard, Water, hot/reactive/unsupported or foreign material refuses; unknown residency or foreign-body state refuses. Their absence must be owner-attested, never inferred from a missing contact. |
| Exact compound | All rectangles have one rigid transform and a disjoint source partition. Overlapping siblings, nonfinite or nonpositive sizes, mismatched payload/geometry and unknown topology refuse before selecting any route. |
| Uniqueness | Pick only a strictly shortest admissible escape within the finite direction family. A second route within `2e-5` cells of the shortest is an ambiguity and refuses. Floating point ordering is not a physical normal. |
| Capacity | At most 64 proxies, 8,192 relevant cell/proxy pairs, eight directions and 32 correction applications. Overflow refuses the whole candidate; no truncation, sparse sampling or expansion after a failure. |

The continuous depth check evaluates the piecewise-linear four-axis SAT depth
over the swept interval, including its extrema; checking only sampled steps
would miss a deepening interior overlap. The fixed `0.25` extra-depth bound is
retained even if the end pose is clear. Equal or incomparable escape choices,
blocked directions, no bounded clearance, or no-new-pair violations are explicit
outcomes. A negative certificate establishes failure only in this fixed-angle,
finite-direction, distance-bounded family. It is not proof that every curved or
rotating path is impossible. Hard-overlap controls are separately classified.

## Mechanical and positional-work contract

The proposed projection is an explicit kinematic constraint repair, not a
normal impulse. It changes position without inventing a restitution velocity or
torque. Instantaneously unchanged velocity does not make it a zero-work event.
For actual mass `m`, actual gravity vector `g` and applied correction `dx`,
record kinetic change `dK`, signed potential change `dU = -m * dot(g, dx)`,
all normal impulses and their separate energy loss, and the positional action.
Ordinary gravity integration, damping, hard collisions and cellular coupling
have distinct before/after states and may not be attributed to the correction.

The newly proposed finite repair reservoir is initialized at most once for an
admitted initial-penetration token/generation/epoch. Its force scale is the
retained lab acceleration `a_ref = 980 * 0.094 = 92.12 cells/s^2`, and its
initial energy ceiling is `E_cap = m * a_ref * 4.0`. Each applied translation
spends `E_spent = m * a_ref * length(dx)` from this reservoir, regardless of
gravity direction, including zero-gravity controls. Record the remaining
reservoir and residual dissipation `E_spent - dU - dK`. A negative residual
beyond the unchanged numerical tolerance, nonfinite result, larger gravity
magnitude than `a_ref`, exhausted reservoir or path-distance budget refuses
before mutation or terminates after readback as appropriate.

This reservoir is deliberately labelled **introduced constraint-repair energy**,
not pre-existing physical Sand energy, calibrated granular elasticity or free
work. It supplies a finite accounting model for otherwise invalid embedding;
an accepted geometric path is not approval of its physical plausibility. No
new reservoir is created by retries, stale observations, ordinary publications,
readback failure, toggling the option or a failed application. The path-length
and energy accounts persist for the complete body ownership lifetime. The
existing per-impulse `1e-8` and actual readback `1e-4` energy limits stay intact.
