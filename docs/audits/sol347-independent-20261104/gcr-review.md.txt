Both arms are hard-clear at tick900 (control first hard proximity 1123), native blocked attempts still occur, and flat/no-event C/E0 is an exact null. This is causal evidence for the event-channel bundle in that population. The intervention jointly removes event impulse/torque, its budget charge and post-event provisional velocity before the passive solve; it does not separate these three mechanisms.

The E0 bank still has six tail overlap cells and correction in 150/300 tail observations. High-barrel tail spans increase roughly 0.831 x 0.476 to 1.715 x 0.918 cells. Thus prevention of large burial is not cleared or quiet rest.
