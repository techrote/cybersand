aggregate-specific #364 contact path. The unchanged planner can reject a valid,
approaching, dissipative frozen contact because `_energy()` first calls

reaches this false refusal while preserving the original six-proxy, 520-cell

defect caused any specific live refusal or six-second burial; live exact contact
and response inputs are absent from the retained trace. Synchronized #364 replay

## Physical limitations and discriminating observations

1. **Material relocation and body resistance are separate mechanisms.** Conservative
   relocation admits only local vacancy paths of 2–5 positions, four cardinal
   moves, depth-four fixed scratch, at most 64 attempts/320 reserved coordinates
   per sample. It retains matter when a valid vacancy is unavailable. The older
   shared 11/9 bridge still supplies its ordinary reaction/correction. Candidate
   Sand resistance is a separate opt-in normal-impulse channel, not a new material
   transport solver or imported GCR force law.
2. **Positive geometry is not the same as an actionable contact.** Native observation
   measures the whole unit-square overlap against all six oriented proxies.
   Only ambient supported Sand whose square crosses an actual exterior segment
   supplies response. Internal sibling seams are removed. A deeply embedded square
   can have substantial area/depth but zero response segment. A response repair
   cannot infer arbitrary new forces from those observations without a new contract.
3. **The current impulse does not recover existing penetration.** For inward unit
   normal n, lever r, mass M and inertia I, the current candidate uses
   j=−v_n/(1/M+(r×n)^2/I), only while approaching. In exact arithmetic
   ΔE=−v_n²/[2(1/M+(r×n)^2/I)]≤0. It is a velocity constraint, not positional
   separation. At zero approaching speed it contributes nothing. Four fixed
   sweeps can leave residual approaching contacts; no full convergence guarantee.
4. **Step ordering matters even with zero publication delay.** The session first
   applies the ordinary compound correction/impulses, then validates/applies the
   candidate, then calls `bridge.step()`. Gravity/damping/hard-contact integration
   follows. It is therefore an inference that perfect observation latency alone
   may not prevent further gravity-driven entry; that mechanism needs stagewise
   measurement. The earlier 12-step mechanism control uses zero gravity and a
   4×2 single-rectangle mechanical control of mass520, not the live six-proxy tree.
5. **Safe refusal is neither successful contact nor actual energy violation.**
   Candidate responses require exact publication identity, age≤1 sample, ≤0.25
   cell maximum vertex drift, authenticated source witness and surviving current
   intersection. One refused record rejects the whole preflight; rejected plans
   apply no impulses. The consumed observation is recorded before the ordinary
   coupling gate, and a rejected ordinary result prevents candidate evaluation.
   Capture those upstream reasons, not merely the candidate refusal histogram.
