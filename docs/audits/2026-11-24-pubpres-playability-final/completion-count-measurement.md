## Prospective exercise decision

Before any count comparison was executed, native, presentation, qualification
and lead review distinguished **exercising the bounded hybrid policy** from
showing that its count condition became eligible before its clock condition.
On the documented83ms Main example, the first newer packet can be deferred;
the next real completion can replace it and make both conditions true at once.
That removes an accepted intermediate transaction even though the scheduled
deadline is also overdue. Calling that observed deferral/replacement a no-op
would incorrectly discard the intended mechanism.

Both count2 arms must therefore show real deferred turns, replaced cumulative
offers, and an accepted anchor independently joined to two genuine completions
beyond the preceding accepted anchor. The original complete physical outcome
parity remains required. Reports separately retain mutually exclusive accepted
eligibility populations: count-only, both, deadline-only, and neither for initial
or immediate paths. A separate cohort field distinguishes observed coalescing
from no coalescing. Every actual upload, offer balance and pending endpoint is
retained. A both-true upload does **not** establish which condition uniquely
caused release. When no count-only observation exists,
`independent_count_before_deadline_attribution` is `NOT_ESTABLISHED`, even when
the bounded hybrid policy was exercised. No-coalescing, deadline-only, invalid
and adverse arms are preserved and do not earn a treatment or performance pass.

This correction was prospective. No previously measured arm, acceptance result,
native parity rule, physical oracle or historical registration was changed.
