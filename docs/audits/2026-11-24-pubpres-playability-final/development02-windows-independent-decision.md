## Prospective disposition

Retain the spare-slot implementation as an opt-in diagnostic with its exact
history, ownership, fallback, allocation and package-smoke gates. Retire repeated
slot-performance and sustained-slot3 captures from the next finite campaign:
Linux showed no strict gain and both Windows matched cohorts are adverse. A
new unrelated native implementation does not answer a new texture-reuse
question. Reopen that performance arm only after a causal renderer change or a
specific physical-driver question warrants it. This disposition does not alter
the original registration, failed workflow or negative data.

Continue ordinary Full2 source and sustained controls, the separately registered
material-deadline and eight-actor mixed workloads, and the new verified font
engine's same-executable owner comparison. These historical captures contain no
new submitted-geometry or external process-CPU sidecars; those values remain
unavailable and require the prospective observer calibration. Physical NVIDIA
acceptance and owner input-to-visible assessment remain unperformed.
