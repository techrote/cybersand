## Design choices

Each choice is experimental and reversible in this dependent branch.

- **Use main's workbench package for its own arm.** D1 selects this existing law.
  Modifying the legacy bearing gate would test a different proposition. Selecting
  a legacy arm reverses this; changing either law requires a new registration.
- **Keep the World that performed the cut and its one body.** The diagnostic
  attachment adds workbench machinery to that World. Opening a new standard
  workbench scenario would replace material authority. Reversal needs a separately
  admitted handoff contract, not a second authoritative representation.
- **Keep main's completion fence in workbench and historical asynchronous pacing
  in legacy controls.** Weakening the fence would not test the adopted package.
  This compares law plus pacing and overlap handling, not force arithmetic alone.
  A common-pacing causal control needs a new preregistered experiment.
- **Disable SOL281 Sand and separate ordinary application in the workbench arm.**
  Main's packet already includes its legacy, granular, passive and correction
  terms. Retaining the old second application would double-count. Reversal would
  create a new combined law and needs fresh scientific registration.
- **Use main's workbench overlap handling in that arm.** Legacy controls retain
  conservative displacement. Splicing the new wrench onto the old displacement
  mechanism would change the adopted package. Native material authority, extent,
  quantities and Water policy stay fixed; reversing this requires a separate arm.
- **Preserve both main's material/arrival algorithms and SOL's ownership and
  serialization refusals.** Two changed protected files receive reviewed
  per-file transitions. Removing either side would discard an admitted contract;
  reversal needs explicit source/material/ownership review. No frozen hash moves.
- **Leave post unused and default pre; apply review F4–F6.** Default calls avoid
  the post-only deep copy, retired envelopes retain their own counters, and an
  unexpected deferred application rejection after a step fail-stops publication.
  Reverting these would reintroduce reviewed overhead/accounting/failure risks.
- **Publish only a dependent Draft.** This exposes the result without integrating
  the historical SOL stack or changing accepted defaults. Promotion and any
  storage/restoration composition remain separate owner actions.

Mechanical document conflicts preserve both routes. Native reconciliation keeps
main's two-argument transient configuration, complete contact-query functions,
failed-world quarantine, occupancy and arrival retirement, plus SOL mutation
fences and physical-hash/CYPS refusal when aggregates cannot be represented.
The worker retains both main emission handling and native representation fences.
