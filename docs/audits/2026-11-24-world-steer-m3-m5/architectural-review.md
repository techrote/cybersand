# Architectural construction review and corrective iteration

**The six development examples completed native construction, but visual and material-plane review found detached service assemblies in five of them.** The corrections now produce six connected static material projections while preserving every declared clearance cell. Corrected native execution and actual Godot rendering remain gates for the hosted qualification campaign.

This is a technical and architectural review with knowledge of the implementations. It is not a blinded preference study, owner acceptance or a model experiment. The six examples remain separate from the frozen thirty-attempt matched comparison.

## Original native findings retained

The original development manifest contains six actual native material planes and eighteen completed construction stages. Independent reduction checked every retained native receipt/readback row against the stage's before/after material bytes, receipt hashes and counter identities, and verified the full World material hash with zero material outside the selected crop. Every declared clearance remained empty.

Every added detail cell touched a real prior structural/service face. Despite that local success, the larger service assemblies could remain detached: housings floated below the hangar roof, the arcology pods had no visible support to the base, a gallery stopped short of its walls, and several service/cavern fittings were isolated. **Local face attachment alone did not establish whole-assembly continuity.**

The original manifest and stage records have an empty `source_revision`. They are preserved as **source-unbound development evidence**, not final source qualification. Thirteen original files, including all six material planes and diagnostic material-ID PNGs, are frozen in `development-support-before/`. These diagnostic images were reviewed for geometric composition; they do not establish graphical shader qualification.

## Remaining architectural and acceptance limits

The six compositions retain true side elevation, broad negative space and sparse contextual detail. They demonstrate reusable construction patterns at two region sizes. Their material geometry remains simplified: stepped rock and orthogonal biological containers have limited organic character, and the pipe/service shapes do not establish working fluid systems. Static corridors do not establish actual actor traversal, access to every room or gameplay quality.

The hosted campaign must execute the corrected recipes on the frozen integrated source, verify exact native material readback and provide actual Godot-rendered evidence. All nine native composition frames, including the separate module-built examples, require review against their retained source/material identities. Linux, Windows and extracted Windows evaluator results remain separate from physical owner acceptance.

The final decision packet should cite those later campaign artifacts when available and retain this development history. No model calls or training occurred in this review. Model-dependent conclusions require authorized genuine model execution; architectural preference and physical gameplay remain owner review gates.
