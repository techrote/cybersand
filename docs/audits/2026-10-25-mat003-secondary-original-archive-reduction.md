S2A quiet E16/E8 comparisons sample an E8 clear but no E16 clear; they are not long-run epoch-cost measurements.

C128's direct E8 medians versus 16-byte C80-aligned: active U128 **0.99153891**, active DUAL64 **1.00267694**, quiet U128 **1.23427624**, quiet DUAL64 **1.00946074**; direct active U128/DUAL64 **1.00594247**. There was **no registered direct quiet U128/DUAL64** or C128/P2 contrast. Independently splitting the seven raw quiet-U128/aligned pairs at the registered completed-tick-256 clear frame gives aligned/U128 individual clear medians **6.016458/8.097801 ms**, clear-frame paired ratio median **1.349024**, paired excess **1.907592–2.191277 ms** and ordinary-255-tick ratio median **0.997184**. The clear frame explains **98.18–105.02%** of each pair's total 256-tick sampled-duration difference. This localizes a frame-level difference; it does not instrument or causally isolate the clear loop, compiler mechanism or cache traffic.

WCLEAR reproduces **336 actual event ticks**: **84 natural E8**, **252 prepared E16/E24/E32**, with 48 distinct contexts × seven real pairs. Largest event-inclusive tick **4.739428 ms**; maximum paired event-minus-ordinary excess **2.509726 ms**. All **112** 2048² pair excesses exceed the historical 1 ms *review trigger*; one negative excess (**−17,425 ns**, E32/512²/active-w1, pair 5) is retained. Width changes frequency, not an individual full-resident hitch; prepared wide-rollover cases cannot be presented as natural long-run execution.

At 2048² resident cells, **array-only** storage is 32 MiB C64, 36 MiB compact C72, 40 MiB compact C80, or 64 MiB at 16-byte stride (aligned/U128/DUAL64). Fixed-array-budget cell capacity relative to C64 is 1, 8/9, 4/5 and 1/2, respectively. This is arithmetic, not fixed-RSS/fixed-workload speed evidence; dense split carries additional allocation/metadata. All historical primary timings belong to the registered Ubuntu24.04/GCC13.3 contexts; they are not Windows/Godot/Wasm performance comparisons.

## Scientific disposition and unclosed gates

**Accept the four historical numerical measurement records as reproducible in their exactly frozen scope.** Retain production **C64/E8**. If an adopted state schema requires +8/+16 logical bits, preserve **C72/C80 P2 safe-memcpy** as an experimental small-extension option; if substantially more genuinely dense authoritative state is required, preserve **DUAL64** as an experimental larger option. Original byte-scatter packed implementations and native U128 are disfavoured by the recorded contrasts, not universally prohibited. Physical lane/memory choice, epoch width and GEN-006's versioned durable semantic record are independent decisions. The reported source/assembly differences support hypotheses, not an instruction-level causal attribution.
