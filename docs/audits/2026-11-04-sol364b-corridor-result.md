cannot safely clear any of the original nine-cell, stationary or rotated initial

different experiment. No accepted case is manufactured by deleting an original

## Why the registered route fails

The candidate admits at most eight signed world/body axes, with fixed orientation,
at most 0.125 cells per correction, at most 32 applications and four cells total.
It uses the actual six source rectangles, 520 unique native members and both
holes. Exact rectangle/unit-cell overlap and continuous SAT intervals determine
the sweep. No previously disjoint rectangle/cell pair may become overlapping,
including another sibling taking over a cell. Hard/excluded initial overlap,
unknown eligibility, capacity overflow, near-tied shortest routes and unsafe
depth growth refuse the complete plan before mutation.

presented as a gross hard-body penetration. The independent distance failure

## Work and ownership limits

The registered candidate explicitly introduces a finite repair-energy reservoir,
`E_cap = mass * 92.12 * 4`, and would spend `mass * 92.12 * |dx|` for each
translation. It records signed gravitational potential change `-mass * g dot dx`,
kinetic change and residual dissipation separately. This is proposed introduced
constraint-repair energy, not measured stored Sand elasticity and not free work.
The per-impulse `1e-8` and actual readback `1e-4` energy limits remain unchanged.
Adversarial arithmetic tests cover zero/down/up gravity, finite/overflow limits,
insufficient reservoir, exhausted distance/application budgets and post-consumption
refusal. They do not validate physical plausibility or a live energy receipt.

Every original target refuses before application, so applied position work,
introduced energy, displacement and kinetic/potential change from the candidate
are all zero. No main-thread RID application seam, native certificate, consumed
sample lease or recovery toggle is added merely to demonstrate an unsafe move.

Current `cybersand.sol281.sand-contact.v1` packets contain exterior witnesses and
aggregate totals, not complete interior and swept-space authority. `complete=true`
does not attest absence of hard, hot, foreign or unresident obstacles. Historical
material RLE cannot reconstruct missing temperature/state/mask/residency values.
A later live route must obtain those values from the actual owner, preserve
bounded capacity and immutable scalars, and hold validity through main-thread
application. Comparing a new certificate only with a cached snapshot or accepting
age at most one does not freeze a concurrently advancing World. Owner-held
revision validity or a serialized apply/acknowledgement protocol is still required.
No worker may inspect live Rapier state or call a Godot API.
