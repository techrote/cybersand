inclusion controls, explicit transmission authorization, Inspect/Copy actions,
the prompt and the send control inside the ordinary Art window. The Proposal

the earlier collapsed-reader presentation defect at the observed window size.

## Creative judgment and remaining owner work

The examples support a useful vocabulary of silhouettes, framed spaces, repeated
elements, contrasting inserts and selected preserved areas. They are editable
native starting points. Their restricted palette, broad empty bays and sparse
detail remain apparent. They should not be described as completed high-fidelity
environments, a demonstration of physical plausibility, or evidence that a model
chose the right architecture from an ambiguous brief.

The matched direct and proposal arms deliberately use equivalent geometry. That
comparison measures execution and owner-control behavior, not artistic novelty.
The owner-operated real-model cohort should assess whether admitted native
context leads to better decisions, whether revisions preserve intended passages,
and whether material/style variety remains useful without obscuring legibility.
Those judgments require actual distinct model proposals and owner ratings; they
cannot be inferred from this deterministic fixture gallery.
