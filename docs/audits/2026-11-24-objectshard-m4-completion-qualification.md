Supported structural materials are Wood, Wall, RedBrick, Concrete,
GraniteBlock, IndustrialBrick, ReinforcedConcrete, SteelPlate, PaintedSteel,
CorrugatedSteel, SteelGrating, CeramicTile, ChemicalGlass and DarkGlass.
Boundary-rooted support replaces the old assumption that any residual Wall
cell anchors an entire component. Severed Wall caps therefore travel with
the detached structure instead of becoming invisible permanent anchors.

The candidate allows 16 material bodies, 16,384 cells per body bounding box,
65,536 aggregate bounding cells and 512 exact collision rectangles per body.
The supported canvas extent is 64 through 1,024, within the World limit of
1,048,576 cells; ordinary authoring uses 512 or 1,024. The ordinary 2,048 default
is deliberately unchanged and attachment there refuses. GCR uses obstacle
IDs 1–48 and mutable material bodies use 49–64.

Attached fluid relocation has a 64-cell search bound and a 1,048,576-probe
transaction budget. The independent Lab retains its eight-cell policy. The
deep Water/Sand fixture exercises the original fluid population; the passing
result is not obtained by deleting fluid or relaxing conservation.

## Completion ordering and independent witnesses

Every physical interval executes real Rapier segments. Each segment reads the
actual body RID, obtains a native pose acknowledgement and checks the exact
native echo before continuing. The final completion command pins the exact
Main GCR publication and request set under the existing ownership mutex.
Native preparation of that final sample occurs while the motion lease is
still held. One due native tick then consumes that prepared packet; neither a
newer publication nor a stale turn-local packet replaces it.

Ordinary actor requests are included in that same completion boundary.
Requests admitted later remain queued. Internal lease waiting does not call
the Equipment or actor-effect adapters as though the user had paused.
Actual user Pause still cancels through the existing generation/in-flight
authority and stops autonomous payload aging. No separate Equipment or
Actor Compose law was changed.

These ordering repairs followed retained negative results, rather than
retroactive reinterpretation of earlier positives. The previous campaign's
Tower stall and the genuinely reproduced suspended equipment request remain
documented in the [negative-result audit](2026-11-24-objectshard-m4-completion-negative-results.md).
The repaired delayed-completion fixture passed 108 checks. The final genuine
equipment/effect fixture passed 99 checks, including a Rocketjumper surface
blast with four actual native material writes and no duplicated next-interval
effect. Independent review recounted its raw world and owned material and all
262,144 projected pixels; the
[review](objectshard-m4-completion-20261124/actor-work-review.json) preserves
the complete clock, request and source identities.

A full continuing-chemistry witness follows an original Wood cell through
480 native ticks and 1,920 real physical segments, until its complete
240-count period-two lifetime expires. Mass changes from 9 to 8 and the new
geometry revision reaches the actual physical body. A separate four-cell
body is cut to zero, with a real positive last-collider query before removal
and continued progression afterwards. The final development run passes 231
checks. This establishes natural cell exhaustion and terminal retirement;
neighbouring Fire can reignite Wood, so whole-building burnout is not promised
within a fixed number of ticks.

The lifecycle adversary independently samples the worker's actual native
clock on every attempted admission. Its final development result contains
118 safe OwnerBusy refusals followed by the exact stale installed-publication
refusal, with all native/actor/Rapier/material clocks fixed. It also checks
malformed, stale and duplicate census reads, unread old tokens across Restore,
and raw conservation. These 122 checks do not rely on Main's cached native
clock while Main polling is deliberately disabled.

## Performance decision and limits of inference

**HOLD until physical Windows/NVIDIA evaluation.** Final source-specific
platform measurements will be appended here after independent review.
Earlier exact-source hosted llvmpipe profiles measured 6.65–7.50 renderer
submissions/s and 1.61–2.52 native ticks/s. The same run's intact paused control
was also only 6.51 submissions/s with no simulation or snapshot work. Earlier
local paired controls likewise remained slow, including paused windows. They
establish a real software-rendered presentation cost, but do not identify
the cause of the owner's NVIDIA result or prove physical display smoothness.

The performance changes preserve required physics and ownership barriers.
They remove redundant full World rasterization, compose only occupied shard
cells, reuse still-valid occupancy receipts, preserve unchanged geometry and
textures, and avoid redundant steady-state readiness commands. The Main pump
is bounded to eight milliseconds and 48 operations.

In the independently reviewed mixed development profile, the entire worker
snapshot stage was 1.533 seconds of 12.365 seconds elapsed, or 12.4%. Even
eliminating that complete stage could save no more than that measured share
of that workload. A material-revision-only payload cache is unsafe because
scheduler due times can change without a material revision. No such cache
was installed. This is a measured upper bound, not an isolated function
benchmark or a prediction of Windows FPS. See the
[cost review](objectshard-m4-completion-20261124/snapshot-cost-review.json)
and [rendered decision](2026-11-24-objectshard-m4-render-decision.md).

## Questions requiring the physical owner machine

The following cannot be closed by a headless test or a cloud software GPU:

1. Does the exact evaluator open on the owner's Windows/NVIDIA driver and
   select the reported renderer/adapter correctly at the intended resolution
   and display settings?
2. With one, three and eight active bodies, are displayed motion and ordinary
   paint/camera/tool response acceptable? Record visible freezes and frame
   pacing separately from native semantic ticks and engine render callbacks.
3. Do substantial sections topple convincingly after support removal,
   including residual offcuts, and do small moving-cell cuts avoid visibly
   implausible angular jumps during real play?
4. Do moving combustion, Water contact, repeated edits, Pause/Play and reset
   remain usable in a longer unscripted session on that machine?
5. Are the scoped authored Save/Restore behavior and bounded refusal messages
   understandable during ordinary use?

Record the package hash and source/runtime manifest, scene, camera, body count,
display resolution, renderer/adapter and NVIDIA driver. Export diagnostics
during active motion and again while paused through **Structures… → Save
diagnostics**. A short screen recording can establish visible toppling,
small-cut response and intermittent presentation stalls. The panel's **Open
diagnostics folder** locates those files directly.

No result here authorizes a merge, Ready promotion, default activation or
unqualified production runtime publication. The material compatibility gate
remains in force. The existing documentation workflow omits the production
runtime-provenance check for Draft PRs; the local full check still exposes the
retained binaries' stale source inputs. Candidate runtime custody is checked
separately and must not be used to claim that production provenance passed.
