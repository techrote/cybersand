## Design choices

- RedBrick43 joins through faces. Native Wall1, Hard wall58 and canvas edges
  anchor the entire component; Sand and ordinary FreeMass Water do not bond it.
  Wood, reactive/mixed members and uncertain contacts are not guessed eligible.
- An erase stroke triggers discovery. Painting alone does not free objects.
  Cold requested-but-unapplied regions refuse; one ordinary Step or Play applies
  them. The handoff never hides a native tick or changes eligibility to pass.
- Keep the same native World and exclusive owner. Stage one inactive main-thread
  body, validate the whole prospective roster, retire exact source tuples,
  install matching terrain and finalize/archive custody. Existing body RIDs,
  pose, motion, M/I and physics parameters survive an append.
- Keep one pending transaction and eight fixed active records. Preserve complete
  native tuples and exact rectangle unions, with a1,024-member/64-square padded
  scope and eight-proxy cap per piece. Every refusal retains the intentional
  erase while leaving the remaining source native. Refusal is not clearance.
- Re-prime the actual unchanged/current roster after every admitted worker cut,
  including zero-release and begin-capacity refusal. Play retains the completed
  native sample fence. Step obtains a fresh paused result and uses the existing
  one-Rapier/one-native-step route. Retired packets cannot acquire new authority.
- Version4 starts add exact rectangular erasures alongside the original discs
  and fresh body records. Versions2/3 remain readable. Save/Restore records an
  authored start, not a serialized live World or complete replay.
- Retained World/roster replacement refuses while captured custody is live.
  Explicit Restore/Load/New blank preflights the authored recipe and uses the
  existing whole-scene fresh-session restart. It retires the complete old owner;
  physical hashing/CYPS guards remain intact. No active #365 restoration occurs.
- Full source-retirement publication occurs once per accepted piece. Other
  protocol stages use normal immutable patches. Command and publication work,
  including refused/duplicate seeds, stays in the evidence denominator.
