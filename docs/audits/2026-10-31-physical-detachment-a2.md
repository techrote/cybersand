## Preregistered protocol

The [machine protocol](physical-detachment-a2-2026-10-31/protocol.json) fixes hash
inputs, limits, strata, seed and decision criteria before acquisition. The same
600-tick real body/Sand fixture is used. A new diagnostic stream retains every
pre-relocation capsule before search-hash deduplication, with 256 pending and 8192
lifetime events; explicit loss blocks a complete census. The original capture
mode and production hash remain available. All ejection geometry, ordering,
path reasons, relocation, masks and incarnation fences are preserved.

The primary identity covers every scientific B/C input, including exact geometry
and complete copied interior plus ring. A coarser translation-invariant structural
signature distinguishes discrete layout/directional diversity from continuous
pose/state fragmentation. Volatile owner/tick/serial and scheduling epochs are
excluded from grouping; raw capsules remain immutable and raw conservation tests
are unchanged. Integrity hashes over entire capsules are not scientific identity.

Bottom-k retention uses 24 unique states per phase/penetration/cardinal-normal
stratum and 16 successful controls. It has no early-arrival preference. The union
is not claimed unbiased. If the complete event stream fits its hard limit, B/C
will also census every unique scientific class and report exact event-weighted
descriptive results. Thus neither finite-run decision depends on sample inclusion.

A strong negative requires at most 1% additional recovery among no-target states
and events, without a predeclared physical subgroup of at least 20 unique states
reaching 10%. At least 10% aggregate is an unexpected experimental signal requiring
independent review; intermediate results remain inconclusive. These are finite
cohort decisions, not runtime acceptance or population confidence claims.

Before data collection,15 new identity/sampling tests and unchanged 10Stage-B plus
15Stage-C tests pass. Read-only review found and corrected structural translation
fragmentation, pending-versus-total producer bounds, input hard-link protection
and ingestion fencing. No physical outcome has been used to tune the protocol.

ejection/no-target at 69. Incoming vertical speed is 91.9 pixels/s; three support
