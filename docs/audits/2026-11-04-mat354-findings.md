## Disposition and scope

an independently executed **mutual-heating law**, and **legacy stochastic resting
behavior**. A quiet-threshold-dependent final image alone would not distinguish
them. This investigation combines source inspection with native kernel counters,
actual scheduler jobs, copied activity/deadline state, legal-destination and
thermal eligibility, physical-state trajectories and independently authenticated
raw evidence.

## H1: actual omitted cooling visits

The kernel uses a staggered four-tick thermal lane, stream192. On an eligible
cold visit it decrements the existing countdown and converts zero to Glass. The
existing pending-wake helper retains compact-pair and Ice obligations, but no
intrinsic MoltenGlass cooling deadline. The source mismatch is only a hypothesis
until the native observations below show the missing work.

All isolated full-clock cases start with one enclosed MoltenGlass cell at
`a=180,b=0,stored_temperature=200`; walls prevent motion. No visible hot neighbor,
body mask, region exclusion or external edit explains the missing visit.

| Phase / coordinates | Default last entry; final countdown at768 | First valid cooling tick omitted with0 jobs/entries/deadline | Omitted cold due ticks through768 | Quiet1024 Glass tick | Active-neighbor Glass tick |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 / `(11,8)` | 3;180 | 4 | 192 | 720 | 720 |
| 1 / `(8,8)` | 4;179 | 5 | 191 | 717 | 717 |
| 2 / `(10,8)` | 5;179 | 6 | 191 | 718 | 718 |
| 3 / `(26,8)` | 6;179 | 7 | 191 | 719 | 719 |

At the first omitted lane the source's block is inactive with quiet3 and due0;
the actual World dispatches no job and the native subject entry count is zero.
Both awake controls execute the unchanged rule and finish exactly on its
predicted180th cold lane. This distinguishes scheduler omission from an
unchanged render buffer, a not-yet-eligible phase or ordinary completed rest.

Short-countdown translations reproduce the mechanism at activity/core/chunk
boundaries and negative coordinates. Stored temperature−300 does not change the
rule or rescue cooling. Removing a hot partner before step17 wakes the default
source and permits another decrement, but it sleeps again after tick20 at179;
awake controls convert at733. This demonstrates loss of the continuing intrinsic
obligation, not failure of the immediate partner-edit wake.

Converted Glass and source-removal controls cease MoltenGlass work. Source-mask
removal, genuine partner unmasking and region re-entry cause actual execution in
the registered cases. Owner pause omits `World::tick`; it neither advances the
thermal clock nor promises catch-up. Fresh reset reconstruction preserves the
registered physical/scheduling state, with the intentional independent
hard-surface revision excluded from the fresh-world equality assertion.

The MoltenGlass-specific predicate is **Fire, Lava or MoltenGlass** in visible
Moore neighbors. It does not include Spark, although generic `is_hot()` does.
On the thermal lane this predicate assigns180; otherwise the cell cools. The
stored temperature field is observed but is not the input to this law. This
investigation does not substitute a thermodynamic temperature-exchange model.
