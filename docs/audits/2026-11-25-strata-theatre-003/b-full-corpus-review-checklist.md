## Inspect lens and movement without inferring speed

Check dense-aperture high-frequency evidence and actual wheel/truss detail across allowed focus and radius changes. A blurred but stable silhouette and a changing alias pattern are different outcomes. Compare the captured quality tier; Diorama/High is an explicitly more expensive optional treatment. Earlier sparse-kernel lens scores do not qualify this revision.

Inspect all four original motion sequences: Classic, Gameplay Clarity, Cinematic Deep Focus and Diorama, ten frames each. Verify actual camera/picking witnesses, three published actor bounds, advancing native/Rapier ticks and unchanged decorative material hashes. Native Play may evolve between frames. The offline sequence's three-frame-per-second playback is an arbitrary inspection cadence; framebuffer readback interrupts execution and establishes neither actual movement speed nor rendering FPS.
