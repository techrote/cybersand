## Underworks: ownership and comparison — about 5 minutes

Open **Strata Theatre… → Scenes**, select **Underworks / Reactor Service**, then
**Load selected example…**. Start with **Optics → Classic**. At a useful zoom,
identify the platforms and openings you think the actor can use. Check your
interpretation with **Layers → Play · actors and collision → Solo**.

**Uncheck Solo**, then select **Stack · all visible planes**. Choosing Stack
alone does not clear Solo. Compare **Gameplay Clarity** and **Cinematic Deep
Focus** at the same camera position. Which false platforms became easier to
reject? Which wheel, brace or machinery detail did you lose?

Try **Bypass / before** and **Processed share**. Before continuing, turn Bypass
**off**, set Processed share to **1.0**, and leave **Inspection focus · none**.
**Classic** changes the authored profile; Bypass temporarily preserves it.

## Optics Readability Challenge: deliberately misleading geometry — about 6 minutes

Save first if needed, then load **Optics Readability Challenge**. Compare
**Classic**, **Gameplay Clarity** and **Noir**. Inspect the false platform
extension, rear stair/ladder and apparently blocked opening. Use Play Solo to
check ownership, then **uncheck Solo and return to Stack**.

Try **Show physical Play** with Bypass off and Processed share at 1.0. It provides
bounded decorative suppression; it does not erase every decorative mark. Play
Solo is the definitive geometry inspection. Turn assistance off after comparing.

Use **Play / Pause** for ordinary live play. Watch the actor body, Water and
falling Sand at both a close and a wider zoom. Is the body itself visible, or
only its label/marker? Do controls respond and materials advance consistently?
Record stalls separately from appearance. **A displayed FPS is not physical
acceptance.** The loaded challenge has one ordinary actor; the gallery's separate
three-actor motion fixture should not be mistaken for this default scene.

## Lunar: lens and atmosphere — about 4 minutes

Load **Lunar Terminator Observatory**. Compare **Classic**, **Cinematic Deep
Focus** and **Diorama**. In the Lab, scroll to the **Quality · balanced** menu;
keep quality matched for a preference comparison. If you try
**Quality · high / experimental**, record that change separately.

Select **Optical Lens**, choose **Manual optical distance**, and move **Focus
distance** and **Sharp focus width**. Judge whether architecture remains
recognizable during camera movement. Does the treatment create useful depth,
or merely soften detail? The native edit target should still say Play. Focus
does not change editing authority. Return to your preferred profile afterwards.

## Your preferred scene: live judgement — about 4 minutes

Load your preferred example. Compare Classic with your favourite treatment at
the same view, then pilot the actor and pan/zoom normally. Record one convincing
depth effect and one misleading or distracting case. Check a dark area, a
bright area and an opening if present. Include the exact profile, quality,
camera/zoom and any adjusted values. Preserve an unsuccessful setting so it can
be reproduced. Low quality is another option to inspect, not a guaranteed
performance pass.

## Save, exit and reopen — about 5 minutes

In **Named custom presets**, enter a new name in **Name for this optical
composition**, then click **Save / duplicate as named preset**. This preset name
is independent of the map name.

Move or close the Strata window. Enter a distinct scene name, for example
`optics-owner-review`, beside **Save start [Ctrl+S]**, and click that button.
Exit completely. Run **Open Workbench.cmd** again, enter the **same scene name**,
then click **Load start**. It reads that named start; it is not a file dialog.

Check the four authored layouts, profile and edited optical values. Temporary
bypass, wipe, clarity assistance and inspection focus should be absent. Quality
is an application preference. Save start preserves the authored scene, not the
evolved simulation or native Undo stacks.

## Send useful feedback

Use the package's `OWNER-FEEDBACK.md`. For each concern, include the context from
the table, the expected physical route, what you actually saw, and whether it
occurred paused or during play. A matched Classic/processed capture and a short
ordinary movement recording help more than a general beauty score. Report scene
loading, slider response, actor/material progression and shutdown separately.
Physical Windows 11/NVIDIA and artistic acceptance remain your decisions; this
evaluation does not merge, promote or automatically enable the feature.
