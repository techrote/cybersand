# Specialist A: original Strata 002 visual corpus review

## Evidence boundary

This is an independent engineering and artistic reading of the ten **actual
Windows evaluator captures** listed below: each scene's complete composite and
its Play-only presentation. The captures came from the preserved qualified
Strata 002 evaluator, application
`934cc0c0eef70dad528155e9a92eebe25e2757eb`, archived by
`410ac8e5e613b1454928699dbc9a68cd035f0894`. The original delivery is
[Actions artifact 14978334114](https://github.com/techrote/cybersand/actions/runs/45509477752/artifacts/14978334114).

The reviewer inspected the images at their original 1600×900 resolution. These
are paused overview captures, not evidence about motion, human task success,
new Strata 003 appearance, or physical NVIDIA performance. Play-only visibility
identifies which rendered structures belong to the authoritative plane; actual
collision truth remains the native admission/readback responsibility.

| Vertical Transfer Spine | Repeated alternating shelves, service conduits and stacked architectural bays establish a legible large-scale rhythm. | Every tier includes rear silver cross-bracing near the genuine shelf level, creating apparent connections across a tall shaft. A treatment that looks clean at a close zoom may erase the bay identity at overview; both scales are necessary. |

These are reviewer hypotheses for the comparative campaign, not a claim that
one profile has solved human depth perception. In particular, the current
tonal shader compresses contrast around `0.5`, so dark surfaces can become
brighter before brightness, atmospheric and opacity attenuation. That may
recover valuable detail or weaken hierarchy depending on the scene. Source
inspection alone cannot choose between those outcomes.

## Actor visibility observation

The original overview captures show a small labelled Midge actor. Its body is
much smaller than nearby architectural motifs. The existing optical helper's
initial local reveal reads the published sampled-player rectangle; it does not
automatically cover every physical or prototype actor. Global clarity still
subordinates rear content, but this is not equivalent to independently measured
local actor reveal. Multiple actors, close zoom and movement need the integrated
graphical campaign.

## Disposition

The original corpus contains strong real architectural ambiguity challenges;
synthetic bars alone are insufficient qualification. Preserve these exact
compositions and camera states for before/after inspection. The renderer's
initial balanced hypotheses favour tonal hierarchy and silhouette-preserving
detail, with strong lens effects remaining optional until actual comparisons
and movement evidence justify them. The previous scene-admission misses remain
unchanged evidence; this visual reading does not waive their 45-second target.
