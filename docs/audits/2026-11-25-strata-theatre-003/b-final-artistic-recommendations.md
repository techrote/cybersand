# Final source-bound artistic recommendations

**Specialist B · 26 November 2026 · Draft/HOLD.** Gameplay Clarity is the strongest practical starting point in the inspected corpus. It makes the actual Play structures more dominant while preserving recognizable rear construction. Cinematic Deep Focus is a useful architectural viewing treatment; it retains detail but also retains misleading bright trusses, frames and ladders. Diorama is an optional artistic treatment, particularly effective in the Lunar Observatory stills. These recommendations concern visible composition. The final Linux candidate has a material registered physical-progression regression, so they do not authorize default activation or performance acceptance.

## What the treatments establish, and what they do not

**Tonal hierarchy is the strongest observed cue.** The useful distinction is the relative emphasis of physical edges and rear construction, supported by texture/detail and colour relationships. It is not simply a darker or blurrier background. In low-luminance regions the tonal transform can raise a rear value: C measured the false rear bar's encoded image luminance at 0.3921 in Classic, 0.4039 in Architectural and 0.3963 in Deep Focus, versus 0.2450 in Clarity and 0.2762 in Noir. These are source-bound encoded-image measurements, not linear photometry or proof of human understanding. Exact probe regions and raw values are retained in [C's measurement record](c-readability-measurements-linux.json).

**Construction-rich preference and gameplay judgement remain distinct.** B finds Deep Focus valuable for studying machinery and architecture. C's adversarial challenge scores Architectural and Deep Focus poorly for false-platform and real-passage distinction despite their retained grids. That disagreement is retained. B's five-axis artistic scale and C's independently registered six-axis challenge scale are not numerically averaged.

**Blur alone does not remove an affordance.** A softened rear bridge can still look like a bridge. Underworks and Abyss demonstrate this clearly. The final directional dense filter preserves important long rails and spokes, and its bounded radius is not a photographic circle of confusion or Gaussian sigma. Different pattern orientations attenuate differently. The Lunar still benefits from that balance; Xeno's fine far mineral construction suffers more. Earlier sparse-kernel judgements are historical and are not transferred to this revised implementation.

**The new local lights remain bounded interactive prototypes.** Sunward's warm shafts and cool machinery pools, Xeno's green pools and ceiling light, and Lunar's restrained accents preserve negative space without an obvious stepped-panel artifact in the inspected overviews. Their visible contribution is too understated to establish convincing volumetric light. Bright native green accents and broad material tint must not be attributed to those procedural motifs. No isolated motif on/off comparison was present, and close-view banding remains an owner inspection item. The [light-motif authoring guide](b-light-motif-authoring-and-feedback.md) records exact regions, eight-band/four-rectangle construction, and safe composition adjustments.

**Actor localization is limited by scale.** B's Overview localization scores depend mainly on labels and rings. C's close movement frames resolve the actual mint actor rectangles in all four sampled profiles; Clarity reduces competing rear detail most. That supports a local visibility observation, not a claim that this system makes every actor legible at arbitrary zoom. The 40 motion images are instrumented framebuffer readbacks with evolving camera and native state; a gallery playback cadence is not actual rendering FPS.

**Correct pixels do not imply acceptable progression.** The registered final Linux challenge gave median completed native-tick rates of approximately 80.0% of Classic for Architectural, 81.5% for Deep Focus, 75.1% for Gameplay Clarity and 75.5% for Diorama High. These are material regressions on hosted software rendering. The [independent performance review](c-performance-review-linux.md) preserves the exact observer and bounds. The nine-scene graphical pass and absence of extra native capture activity do not override this result. The original five Strata 002 Windows admission misses remain preserved against the unchanged 45-second target; the new Linux timings are not a hardware-matched replacement for them.

## Owner evaluation and disposition

Start with Classic and Gameplay Clarity at the same camera. Identify a physical route before comparing richer profiles. Use temporary bypass, Clarity assistance and Play/Solo inspection when a rear bridge, ladder or frame still looks physical. Then compare the architectural choices in the table, keeping the same scene and zoom. Lunar Diorama and Xeno Alien are useful contrasting artistic experiments; neither should silently become the presentation of an older document.

Ask separately: **Can I identify the route? Can I still enjoy and inspect the architecture? Does the space feel layered while moving? Does physical progression remain responsive?** A strong answer to one is not a substitute for the others. Record the scene, profile, quality, zoom and exact troublesome structure. The [owner guide addendum](OWNER-GUIDE-ADDENDUM.md) supplies precise Solo, scrolling and named Save/Load instructions while preserving the frozen evaluator guide's identity.

**Artistic disposition:** coherent optical authoring system with useful tonal hierarchy, retained architectural detail, meaningful environment-specific palette and lens choices, and an independently observed clarity benefit. Persistent false-bridge and false-floor ambiguity, understated local-light evidence, overview actor-size limits and the registered performance failure remain explicit. Final Windows/package verification and physical Windows 11/NVIDIA acceptance are separate pending gates at the time of this review. **Draft/HOLD remains the appropriate disposition.**
