# ObjectShard eight-body visible crop and native motion

The TITAN RTX rendered the fixed eight-body Workbench stress scene for 9.473 seconds. The desktop sampler observed 304 changed images of its 700×253-pixel world crop. All 178 paused comparisons were identical. The capture had zero occluded observations and zero accumulated multi-frame arrivals.

The longest interval between two changed images was 67.146 ms. Three intermediate desktop images were identical. Three engine observations fell wholly inside that interval after applying the clock uncertainty. All eight material bodies moved inside the crop. Their largest adjacent Rapier displacements ranged from 0.481 to 0.551 cells (0.665 to 0.762 screen pixels). The published native tick advanced from 83 to 84 and the Rapier step from 332 to 333.

From the first valid active comparison to the first changed image, a separate 382.679 ms startup interval contained 22 unchanged comparisons. The published tick stayed at 38 while two Rapier steps completed. All eight bodies moved inside the crop, with maximum adjacent displacements from 0.0044 to 0.0798 cells. Every recorded body had visible occupied cell centers at least two world cells inside the nominal crop.
