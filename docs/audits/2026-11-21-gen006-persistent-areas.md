## Measurement interpretation

Capture, durable publication, prepared restoration and actual native-step
durations are recorded separately. Prepared restoration excludes earlier file
reading/validation and final adoption. Capture/publication arrays measure
successful calls; native capture microseconds are a subset of capture time and
must not be added twice. Full operation elapsed time includes the whole path.

Operation records identify travel, Ahead, save and recovery. Native slices pair
actual completed ticks with their own measured microseconds. Injected failures
are labelled independently, and repeated terminals for one owner operation must
not be summed. Recovering ownership after a timed-out save does not mean that
save was published. Process RSS and CPU are measured from the actual engine PID.
One/two/three-area bootstrap samples measure retained encoded payload and process
RSS, not isolated per-area heap ownership. The whole-process maximum main-thread
gap includes qualification layout changes and injected faults.

Development measurements show that durable publication dominates normal travel.
The three-second stepping budget is not a promise that capture, storage, restore
and presentation together finish in three seconds. Final platform reports must
publish travel-only percentiles, actual slice throughput and explicit measurement
scope; waiting elapsed time must not be reported as productive simulation work.
