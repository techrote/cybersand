# OBJECTSHARD-001R — integrated repairs

This child of M2 preserves its compact material owner, real Rapier host and
selected shared chemistry. It repairs the demonstrated admission and settling
mechanisms, and makes the separate Lab useful for sustained exploratory editing.
The source checkpoint is Draft/HOLD. It is not owner physical acceptance or a
production Workbench integration.

## Detachment and scarce resources

`object_shard_discovery.hpp` performs a bounded, resumable census of complete
four-connected Wood/RedBrick components. Ordinary mutation witnesses invalidate
affected topology; same-topology state changes still invalidate exact capture.
Admission waits for a complete census, chooses the largest eligible component
for three admissions, then the oldest waiting eligible component for fairness.
Anchored, resident-boundary, bounding-box, shape, transient-occupancy and body-slot
outcomes are distinct. Unknown or incomplete membership never becomes a shard.

An obstructed large candidate defers for eight preparation calls while other
eligible candidates can progress; it is then reconsidered without requiring a
fake topology edit. Full-slot and immutable geometry refusals reuse the census.
Prepared payloads are still revalidated before ownership commits. Intersecting
accepted explosions remain a refusal; unrelated pending effects retain World
ownership and do not block an otherwise independent promotion.

The [independent connectivity oracle and negative controls](2026-11-22-objectshard-001r-native-admission.md)
reproduce original `[1,4,9]` admission ahead of a 400-cell component, repeated
full-slot traversal and non-progress with a small work budget. Candidate fixtures
admit `[400,9,4]`, resume the search and avoid repeated traversal. These synthetic
fixtures establish the mechanisms; the owner's incomplete B export cannot prove
the exact remaining World topology.

## Real rest and bounded motion

Necessary collider/mass replacement explicitly wakes the Rapier body. The bridge
does not overwrite the solver's required wake with its previous sleeping state.
Unchanged rectangle geometry retains shape handles; mass-only updates preserve
the same collider union. Exact erosion is still installed and can cause a real
fall when support is removed. Natural sleep, collisions and unconstrained motion
remain active. The [forensic control](2026-11-22-objectshard-001r-forensics.md)
retains the original late jump and its controlled repair.

A semantic interval is divided into at most 16 **actual Rapier steps**. Each
accepted segment must satisfy the existing four-cell translation and 0.20-radian
rotation limits, plus the existing corner-travel/sweep capacity. Native occupancy
and historical contact are applied and acknowledged before another segment runs.
The receipt binds session, owner/body generation, geometry, pose serial and the
unchanged semantic tick. Chemistry advances once per semantic interval. No
interpolated fictitious physics, angular wrap alias, clamping or weakened guard
is used. A refused segment holds the isolated session with inventory retained.

## Owner-aware cutting and chemistry

Stationary cuts use World edits. Moving structural cuts use an exact
`ShardEditRequest`, including the observed owner, material/geometry revisions,
body generation, pose serial, cell incarnation and payload. Exact replacement
geometry is capacity-tested before debit. Main installs and acknowledges it
between edits/steps. Cargo and products are not silently treated as structural
Wood. Repeated empty edits are cheap no-ops; the input queue is bounded.

The visible sustained burner authors genuine World Fire cells through the
ordinary material-authoring API. Its accounting is separate from shard-generated
Fire. The accepted Wood burn law, Fire lifetime/transport and Smoke behavior are
unchanged. A selected external Fire contact uses the existing lane and sampled
direction, while Water remains non-consuming extinguishing evidence.

Cross-review found and reproduced two serious intermediate defects before
qualification: one Fire inventory could obtain another opportunity after
mechanical relocation, and a refused motion could discard an earlier illegal
Sand-entry fence. The repair carries a bounded per-World-carrier opportunity token
through relocation, preserves its random-coordinate anchor, and rolls back
contact reservations/history on a nonmutating refusal. A narrowly opt-in World
ignition guard prevents an opportunity already spent on shard Wood from also
igniting World Wood during that same tick. It suppresses only that already-spent
ignition; lifecycle and movement continue. The claim view exists only during the
exclusive coordinator's World tick and is cleared on return or exception.
Ordinary Worlds have no installed claims and retain their previous behavior.

The new token field is shared once by the bounded Lab, not allocated per shard.
Its storage and the reserved refusal-history backup are included in native
reported memory. These additions require measurement against the registered
budgets; correctness is not evidence that a timing or memory gate passes.

## Playtesting and custody

The Lab provides original A–D controls and additional perforated, inert-settling,
large-plus-offcuts, irregular, diagonal-cut, sustained-flame and long-session
presets. Large fixtures use ordinary edits in bounded batches with control-loop
yields. Diagnostics separate current-session native metrics from lifetime
accounting and show the real admission reason, owner, geometry, sleeping state,
mass/COM, angular motion and segment receipts.

Prospective JSONL capture records typed named operations, source/scenario
identity, accepted and refused results, semantic timing, owner/geometry events
and bounded diagnostics. The stream and in-memory queues have explicit caps.
Source-bound replay executes real native/Rapier operations and reports
differences; it does not claim cross-platform solver determinism. The six old
owner exports remain incomplete historical records.

The generated Windows CMD root is `%~dp0.` so its quoted argument does not end
with a backslash. Packaging seals the scripts before hashing. The Windows-only
ZIP qualifier extracts the **same delivered bytes** into spaces, parentheses
ending in `(1)`, long nested and Unicode roots, executes both shipped launch and
verification paths, then verifies the manifest again. Linux synthetic tests do
not count as that Windows execution.
