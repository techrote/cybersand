Specialist B inspected the actual Linux ordinary Workbench full-stack captures for all five scenes from application `316020a4aa87ca7993716f00d714301884d580f7`, plus the orbital distant-plane/detail frames and the underworks atmosphere frame. The lead independently identified the weak orbital and arcology distance cues. They also expose a real design weakness: too many rear elements were isolated rectangular outlines, and several backgrounds contributed mostly near the bottom of the view. Passing material and coordinate tests does not establish convincing environmental art.

## Qualification boundary

These refinements require a new integrated native and framebuffer execution. They are not covered by the first campaign's PASS observations. A scene author's source review cannot substitute for C's independent runtime checks, lead image review or the owner's physical Windows/NVIDIA aesthetic decision.

The first matched atmosphere and Solo comparisons had an independent ordinary Play shader TIME confound. C froze and restored that shader parameter for the next campaign, disabled atmosphere during isolated native-plane captures, and corrected the horizontal actor probe from yaw-only input to ordinary yaw plus thrust. Original negative evidence remains intact. Atmosphere renderer strengths are deliberately unchanged here, so the corrected matched controls can be evaluated before further visual tuning.

The annular machinery is ordinary authored material geometry on a decorative native plane. It is neither moving native material nor a structural or physical load-bearing claim. All revised geometry remains exact integer-cell placement with existing material IDs, within existing extent, per-plane rectangle and authored-write budgets.
