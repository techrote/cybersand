## Design choices and rejected alternatives

The successor keeps the existing private candidate/adoption owner and borrowed Play presentation adapter. Extracting a second generic renderer or running the old standalone host would enlarge ownership and reconciliation risk without helping this scoped workflow. Reversing that choice requires a separate engine integration and new World/actor identity qualification.

Raster conversion remains an explicitly reviewed interpretation with no-write masks. Automatic depth inference and automatic clearing of transparent openings were rejected for this milestone because pixels do not establish depth or collision authority. Native masked modules remain exact-grid data; raster integer replication is a separately declared operation.

Construction commits checked per-plane chunks and reports their actual acknowledged prefix. Cross-plane atomicity, an attachment solver and automatic rollback were not implemented. A future transaction model must provide its own authoritative guarantee instead of relabeling a partial result. Native construction and appearance approval stay separate; applying the latter replaces the reviewed complete atmosphere configuration.

Three static native decorative Worlds plus lightweight visual motifs were chosen over independent dynamic cellular clocks. No physical Steam, Water transfer, decorative actor space or new scheduler is implied. Independent decorative resolutions and caches were considered but not introduced; equal-resolution native identity and existing static change-only publication remain the conservative baseline.
