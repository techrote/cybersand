## Architectural observations

**Orbital Fabrication Terminal.** The distant dock now forms a connected silhouette with upper structural arms instead of a detached outline. Bright Play decks remain easy to distinguish from the darker rear trusses and broad lower flight space. The environment retains a deliberately sparse fabrication-hall character; the presence of distant structure does not establish physical collision or load support.

**Underworks / Reactor Service.** The large native rear turbine and pressure columns give this scene a stronger central machine hierarchy than the earlier hollow-box composition. Bright service platforms remain distinct. The turbine is static decorative native geometry. It is not a physically rotating machine or a hazard merely because its silhouette crosses the apparent flight channel.

**Abyss Foundry.** Continuous far rock along the ceiling and sides gives the cavern a more convincing depth boundary around its central void. Bright industrial platforms, purple mineral edges and dark rear surfaces remain separable. Some small white decorative dust motifs can resemble isolated material pixels at overview; this remains an owner appearance judgment.

**Vertical Transfer Spine.** Rear carriage cages, continuous hoist cables and the pulley make the repetition read as a coherent shaft. Lower rear panels now terminate inside the chamber. Fit-to-world strongly reduces the portrait native grid, so overview is insufficient for judging individual material pixels.

**Rain over the Arcology.** The revised PNG initially exceeded the log audit's 1 MiB transport cap. After executor recovery, B verified the original artifact ZIP (89,677,038 bytes; SHA-256 `0f2c10d46b1a5da76af436f0517090653211a8375e8adeb46730c2e4835b4b4f`) and extracted only selected original PNGs and receipts. Every selected member matched the original qualification ledger. The taller stepped far towers are visible between nearer inhabited blocks and through foreground openings; the isolated Play frame shows large physical flight voids, and the isolated background is a distinct editable native skyline. Uneven roof levels and cyan/magenta/yellow accents give the city a different character from the industrial scenes. Sparse rain segments and low haze are visible in the matched pair. The strong diagonal facade pattern is also present with atmosphere OFF and is not evidence of simulated rainfall. Actual install and fresh-process reopen receipts both have zero failures for this scene.
