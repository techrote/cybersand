## Ownership, scheduling and supported limits

Promotion uses a bounded whole-Lab pause after a healthy returned native tick.
World remains owner while main reserves a frozen, noncolliding Rapier body.
After native commit, actual affected terrain content must be installed and
acknowledged, native occupancy must be applied, and `BodyActivated` must return
before chemistry and motion resume. Queued work is not an applied-state receipt.

Payloads retain material, state_a, state_b and temperature. Scheduler epochs are
remapped; semantic deadlines and logical random anchors prevent duplicated burn
opportunities and motion-based reseeding. Rapier sleep does not suspend chemistry.
Products occupy real bounded carriers; the outbox references that sole inventory.
Blocked delivery retains inventory or follows its advertised lifecycle.

Precommit refusal/cancellation leaves World ownership unchanged. Unexpected
partial progress quarantines exact actual ownership. Session generations,
monotonic transactions and a retired receipt watermark prevent stale messages
and evicted receipts from executing twice. Geometry, mass and generation are
installed together between physics steps. Losing a RID does not delete inventory.

The owner Lab is bounded to three shards, 4,096 local bounding-box cells per shard,
12,288 in total, and 256 exact shapes per body. Discovery, contacts, material
transfers, publication and control receipts have separate finite capacities.
The admitted step envelope is four cells of translation and 0.20 radians, with
bounded half-cell contact sampling and at most 32 samples. Exceeding the envelope
holds/refuses with retained ownership; arbitrary-speed chemical CCD is not claimed.

Supported structural materials are Wood and separately admitted inert RedBrick,
with explicit like-material cohesion and Wall anchoring. Baseline whole-cell Sand
may lodge through a validated one-to-three-cell opening. Water supplies selected
non-consuming extinction evidence; its quantity and solver sidecars stay in World.
Cargo contributes declared occupancy and unit-cell mass without becoming a bond.

Unsupported operations remain explicit: arbitrary Plant or material admission,
consuming Acid, fractional transfer, Wave/PressureFlow/inertial/ballistic sidecar
transfer, general internal transport, fracture, streaming and durable save/resume.
Disconnected islands remain one exact nonfracturing compound, labelled
`split_pending`, while chemistry continues. Large persistent welded islands are
an owner-visible gameplay limitation, not concealed fracture support.

Population accounting separates conservative transfer, authored creation and
destruction, chemistry/lifecycle changes and explicit session-reset disposal.
Water quanta are a separate ledger. No universal stoichiometric mass claim is made.
