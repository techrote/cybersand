perforated, saturated and dormant/wake workloads, but excludes the external
Water/Sand scenario. Workbench mixed/viaduct profiles require relocation, not
Cargo lodging or extinguishing. Thus those profiles alone are not evidence of
the latter two behaviours in the extracted Godot/native/Rapier tuple.

and passed 379 checks on final COM local native `547c8f6f`. Root authorised its

`objectshard-lab-integration-test-v1`, with positive `checks` and integer

## Stationary Tower evidence boundary

The current Tower fixtures do not directly prove an already detached facade
resting on a stump and subsequently falling when that same stump is removed.
The remote-barrel notch leaves another connected support and verifies continued
simulation/barrel state; it does not record first-stage owned-body rest/contact.
Full stem removal and the ordinary Workbench completion fixture demonstrate
actual detachment, fall and toppling. The separate physical box contact fixture
demonstrates valid rest followed by fall after static support replacement.
Remaining physical support is therefore a valid explanation to investigate for
the owner's stationary facade, not an established cause of that observation.
