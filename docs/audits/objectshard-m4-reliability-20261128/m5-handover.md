# ObjectShard M5 handover

This is a development route after physical M4 evaluation, not an implemented M5 architecture. Keep the current body and work budgets until experiments justify a different representation and its conservation contract.

## What this mission established

Ordinary low-inertia collision responses can exceed a fixed angular ceiling without excessive occupied-material motion. Retained empty storage can also invent large origin motion after extensive cutting. The corrected contract separates material-local storage from the physical COM and the occupied-carrier contact domain. The native sweep, Water and curved Sand proofs must continue to share that reference.

The baseline orchestration paid unnecessary callback waits and transported full snapshots for intermediate physical acknowledgements. The candidate services ordered motion receipts under the existing lease, pumps admitted work before the next physics decision, and preserves full final/failure publications. See the final measurements and same-native control in `throughput.md`; do not substitute renderer callback cadence for semantic or display performance.

Native contact/occupancy work and a serial, roster-wide acknowledgement chain remain scaling constraints. Sparse storage is now physically correct, but its traversal costs can still depend on the retained extent. An extra material shader also has a meaningful cold first-use cost on hosted software rendering. None of these observations supports increasing the body budget without measuring the resulting costs.

## Boundaries worth keeping

- The native owner remains the sole authority for material inventory, occupied cells, chemistry and transfers; Rapier supplies physical poses through explicit acknowledgement.
- Main alone owns the physical space. Each semantic interval consumes its admitted physical duration exactly once, including Pause, Step, pending equipment requests and failure paths.
- Material/geometry revisions bind the motion domain. A physical lease fixes that domain; semantic ingress and geometry replacement occur at the owner barrier.
- Full render publications are immutable and identity-bound. Camera inspection of retained state remains independent of simulation progress, including Held operation.
- Supported capacity, resource exhaustion, stale witnesses and unprovable motion have explicit refusal semantics. Future optimizations need equally reviewable failure and rollback rules.

## Recommended sequence

1. **Establish physical owner results.** Repeat the tower, small-fragment, barrel, camera, material and eight-body route on the declared Windows machine. Use recordings to distinguish sustained simulation cost, transaction latency, cold shader preparation and presentation.
2. **Reduce work within the current ownership model.** Measure occupied-mask and contact traversal, cached unchanged footprints, sleeping bodies and sparse retained storage separately. Prototype revision-bound cached motion/contact domains before adding another transaction protocol.
3. **Experiment with batching.** Determine which acknowledgement preparation and native readback work can be batched without losing per-body geometry identity, contact accounting, stale rejection or one-shot time. Keep final publication and actor/equipment ordering explicit.
4. **Design size-dependent representations.** Compare a small rigid fragment with lightweight debris under a common inventory/conservation ledger. Specify transitions, interaction fidelity, recovery and save semantics before targeting hundreds of independently moving pieces. Body-count claims need a representative all-active workload.
5. **Extend interaction and presentation deliberately.** Buoyancy, fluid resistance, granular loading, corrosion and thermal weakening require independent force/material contracts. Presentation interpolation and richer optical integration should consume acknowledged state and preserve camera/scene authority. Strata integration is a separate accepted-baseline decision, not permission to import unfinished rendering architecture.

Storage compaction, hierarchical transactions and hybrid debris are experiments, not automatic next patches. Each should answer a measured bottleneck with a falsifiable correctness and throughput comparison. The small-fragment and padded-storage proofs, exact pose echoes, mixed-material tests and extracted-package checks from this mission form the regression boundary for that work.
