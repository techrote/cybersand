## Registered experiment before tuning

First use the actual native aggregate and Rapier apparatus with the retained
520-cell payload, six occupied rectangle proxies and two holes. Observe every
declared synchronized physics step. Preserve baseline and the current
velocity-only candidate as controls. Use identical frozen contacts with fresh,
missed and deliberately delayed receipt to separate geometry, publication and
response. Geometry measurements use exact oriented rectangle/unit-square
intersection; the bounding rectangle is only a traversal bound.

There are two distinct questions. An interior Sand square can have positive
area without crossing an exterior face. A valid source face can also move
slightly so that its old midpoint leaves the square while a positive exterior
subsegment remains. The latter is a possible unnecessary whole-observation
refusal. Executable counterexamples must precede selecting the correction;
neither code inspection nor asynchronous correlation proves its live frequency.
A dissipative velocity constraint has no authority to remove stationary initial
embedding. If that is the remaining mechanism, record a failed clearance gate
instead of adding unregistered positional work or new impulse energy.

The first admitted repair, if reproduced, is restricted to revalidating the
same already witnessed exterior contact segment at the present pose. It must
not create a new material witness, extend a source segment, fill a hole, cross
a sibling seam, increase the 0.25-cell drift limit, relax an identity fence or
skip validation of a later malformed contact. Velocity, mass, COM, inertia,
four response sweeps and energy bounds remain unchanged. No body position
correction is registered in this slice.

## Newly proposed physical gates

These are prospective experimental limits, not previously owner-approved
gameplay limits. They remain recorded even if the bounded repair cannot meet
them. Parameter tuning must not retrospectively move these thresholds.

| Gate | Proposed limit and basis |
|---|---|
| Contact witness | Every valid clipped current subsegment of the original exterior witness is handled deterministically; its point and weight derive from that subsegment. Invalid, duplicate, internal-seam and out-of-contract observations produce no mutation. |
| New penetration | In a nonembedded controlled 60 Hz impact, maximum depth must remain at most one cell; the one-cell material resolution is the outer transient bound, not a target penetration. |
| Initially embedded depth | Maximum depth must not grow beyond its measured initial depth plus 0.25 cells, the existing maximum admitted pose drift. Initial burial is reported separately and never subtracted from measured depth. |
| Sustained clearance | Within 120 observed 60 Hz steps (two seconds) after first valid impact, Sand intersection area must be no more than 1e-6 cell squared and maximum depth no more than 2e-5 cells, and remain so for at least 60 further observed steps. The depth tolerance matches the existing contact precision and the area tolerance exceeds the native 1e-9 reporting filter. |
| Recurrence | No overlap above those tolerances after the sustained-clearance window. Record maximum, total cell-ticks and longest positive run across the complete trace, including before the window. |
| Stability | Finite pose/linear/angular state on every step, no response kinetic-energy increase beyond the unchanged limits, and no scripted displacement or angular reset. Report actual velocity, rotation, torque and residual approaching contacts; finite values alone are not plausible-motion acceptance. |
| Impulse and work | Existing per-impulse 1e-8 relative/absolute kinetic bound and actual-readback 1e-4 relative bound are immutable. Positional correction work is exactly zero. The existing mass/inertia effective-mass formula supplies impulse bounds, rather than a new tuned force cap. |
| Publication | A fresh synchronized receipt has sample age zero; a one-step delay retains all current-pose checks; age greater than one refuses. Every deliberate missing/delayed publication is counted. Report elapsed latency independently of simulation-step age. |
| Capacity | Preserve 8192 geometry probes, 256 contact records, 64 proxies, all ownership/residency/thermal refusals and complete-or-refused publication. No history cache or unbounded retained contact queue. |
| CPU | All 24 original platform/worker/environment/arm groups must meet the unchanged 2 ms maximum-of-three-means gate: all relevant owner and response attempt work divided by complete consumed observations. No omitted refusal, changed denominator or sampling reduction. |

Synchronized metrics are cell-ticks only when every declared physics step has
an authenticated geometry observation. Deliberately absent delivery does not
permit absent geometry measurement. Asynchronous metrics remain cell-samples
and area-samples; their histories are not matched causal comparisons. Report
before/after ranges, maxima, counts and variance, without estimating unobserved
steps. Elapsed monotonic intervals around work are the existing cost measure;
they must not be misdescribed as operating-system thread CPU accounting.
