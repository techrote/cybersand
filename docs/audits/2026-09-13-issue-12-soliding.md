never gains cohesion. Hole coverage is exact (the ring is rejected by the initial

the narrow-neck geometry demonstrates why rest/packing alone cannot authorize a

support/topology, failed-world status and included coverage witness rest. Same-tick
queries earn none; missing observations, clear/reset, exclusion/re-entry and change-
and-restore events reset it. Terra found mask add/remove ABA between observations;
a monotonic global mask witness and two lifecycle regressions fixed it. Unrelated
mask changes conservatively reset rest, a known false-negative/cost limitation.

Final native runner passes all 130 cases. It reports 60872-byte Observer and

observations. Every observation still visits 1296 witness cells at maximum size.
