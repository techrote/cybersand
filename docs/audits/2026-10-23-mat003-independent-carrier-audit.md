## Non-interchangeable measurement contracts

**S2A.** Active512² with four configured workers for A01–A08 compares *physical layouts* at E8; quiet2048² with one worker for A09–A16 compares each representation at E8 versus E16. All processes are independent and alternated AB/BA; 120 warmup + 256 measured `World::tick` ticks per process, seed204. Primary statistic is each B process's *mean of all 256 ticks* divided by its paired A process mean, then median/min/max/MAD of seven process-pair ratios. Tick nearest-rank quantiles are descriptive, not independent replicates. E8 has actual clear at completed tick256 in the sampled horizon; E16 has **none**. Therefore quiet E16/E8 values are a **short-horizon clear asymmetry**, not long-run epoch cost.

**S2P.** Four **E8 active512-w4** contrasts under the same 120+256 timing definition, diagnostics off, original P1 against defined safe-memcpy P2, and independent C64-word control comparisons. E8 clear remains in every process mean. Seven pairs per contrast are replicates, not 256 ticks; near-unity P2/C64 medians are not an equivalence proof.

**C128.** Five **E8** contrasts, active512-w4 and quiet2048-w1, all 120+256, diagnostics off, neutral high64 and E8 clear included. No direct *quiet* u128/dual64 contrast; no C64 or compact P2 contrast. The registered quiet u128/aligned ratio is 1.23427624. A supplementary, non-primary partition of the retained data gives clear-inclusive frame median ratio 1.349024, paired clear-frame differences 1.907592–2.191277 ms, and ordinary 255-tick median ratio 0.997184. The clear-inclusive frame accounts for 98.18–105.02% of the sampled-duration difference per pair. This **localizes the observed excess to that frame**, not to a separately instrumented clear loop or a demonstrated microarchitectural mechanism.

**WCLEAR.** 48 contexts = E8/E16/E24/E32 × resident512/1024/2048 × active/sleep-excluded × configured workers1/4, seven AB/BA ordinary-versus-event **fresh-process pairs each**, one observed tick per process. 336 true clear events, of which 84 are natural E8 and 252 use prepared E16/E24/E32 stamps; independent natural E16 control exists for *correctness*, not observed performance. Common C80-aligned 16-byte stride, identical resident footprint within size, observer-off but physics diagnostics enabled. The prepared memory rewrite/prescan, initial cache/store state, ordinary comparison and simulation workload are included. Neither paired excess nor full event-inclusive tick isolates the clear loop or predicts natural long-run rollover latency. Among seven events, nearest-rank p95/p99 equal the observed maximum; no population-tail inference is supported.

**No cross-campaign multiplication or chain ranking.** Even when they share a relative control name, S2A/S2P/C128/WCLEAR have separately registered source, execution history, instrumentation, workloads, sample units and physical preconditions. In particular, `S2P P2/P1 × S2A P1/C64` is **not** a measured P2/C64 ratio; S2P already supplies a direct contrast. The C128 aligned control does not create a paired P2/DUAL64 comparison. WCLEAR event costs cannot be subtracted from C128 sample means as matched causal contributions.

## Rollover and fixed-memory interpretation

The first clear for a zero-start E-bit epoch is at tick `2^E`; subsequent clear interval is `2^E - 1` (because cleared counters restart at one). E8 first clear256 then every255; E16 first65536 then65535; E24 and E32 event intervals are16,777,215 and4,294,967,295. WCLEAR measured each prepared event once per fresh B process, not 16m/4bn natural ticks. For each exact context, the [projection JSON](mat003-continuation-2026-10-23/wclear-39889832605-projections.json) uses measured ordinary-tick median N and paired-excess median D to report **conditional** `N + D/(2^E - 1)`, *not* measured steady-state cost, natural high-width event tail, long-running overall p95/p99, or cold gameplay.

Dense cell-array footprints alone, at **2048² fixed cells**: C64 32MiB; packed/memcpy C72 36MiB, C80 40MiB; dense split base+tail 36/40MiB plus allocation/descriptors; padded/aligned/C128 64MiB. At **fixed cell-array bytes**, relative capacity versus C64 is 1 for C64, 8/9 for C72 compact/split, 8/10 for C80 compact/split, and 1/2 for 16-byte storage (not a simulated fixed-memory benchmark). Temperature at 2 bytes per cell contributes an independent 8MiB at2048² when fully allocated, plus other World allocations. RSS and fixed-cell arithmetic cannot be combined into a measured fixed-memory performance ranking. There is no accepted production *end-to-end fixed-budget* benchmark.

## Source and assembly evidence with causal limits

The [continuation report](../research/mat003-continuation.md#s2p-mechanism-and-controls) and retained source/assembly show original P1 packed reconstructs the 64-bit head through eight byte loads/shifts/OR and scatters bytes for partial updates. P2 uses defined 8-byte `memcpy` of the head (little-endian fast path with safe fallback) and a bounded 1/2-byte tail; GCC13.3 emits ordinary word-load/bit-mask/store sequences even though the carrier reports alignment1. Static wrapper instruction counts P1→P2: `state_a` get12→4, set26→9, epoch clear21→4. Generated C72 World `update_cell` static count107→80, `transfer_water`862→621, with no dynamic instruction profile. This substantiates an **avoidable implementation-specific reconstruction penalty**, not a quantified cache/bandwidth/root-cause decomposition.

Retained C128 source/assembly establishes two 64-bit lanes for DUAL64 (16-byte size, 8-byte alignment) and native scalar `unsigned __int128` (16-byte size, 16-byte alignment). On the inspected GCC13.3 output, DUAL64 low-field partial writes/epoch clears operate on its low word; native U128 emitted additional high-lane preservation/load/store work on those wrappers. This is a **plausible mechanism hypothesis**, not proof that those particular instructions, cache associativity, bus traffic, alignment, unaligned penalties, false sharing, compiler register pressure or compiler scheduling caused the 23.43% quiet frame result. No counters/isolated-loop attribution or other compiler performance acceptance exists. C128 whole-high64 correctness was tested separately from neutral-high64 timing; no adopted future high-state workload has been evaluated.
