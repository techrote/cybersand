# Experimental reciprocal Gentek assembly

**Implementation candidate / Draft / HOLD.** [Programme #687](https://github.com/techrote/cybersand/issues/687)
owns this work; [#605](https://github.com/techrote/cybersand/issues/605) owns its
architectural intent. This page describes the experimental branch, not a
production default or physical support certificate. The native stage and the
dependent experimental Workbench stage have separate source and acceptance
receipts. Neither may merge or become Ready automatically.

## Authored components and ownership

The [native value API](../../native/experiments/gentek_swarm/assembly.hpp) has
finite parts, local/world quarter-turn transforms, typed point and segment
sockets, explicit attachments and nested assembly definitions. Expansion rejects
cycles, incompatible or wrongly facing joints, disconnected footprints, hidden
foreign-required exports and exhausted resources. Every accepted claim has one
owner, part identity, authored rule and revision. Canonical weld ownership does
not duplicate a native material cell.

FRAMEWEAVER owns its steel grammar: caps, flush plates, members, elbows, braces,
junctions, brackets, nested trusses and backward receiving bearings. MOSAIC owns
its native masonry categories and receiving responses through the unchanged
GEN-003 sixteen-category, 16-by-16 logical-cell solver. The coordinator chooses
neither masonry categories nor anonymous shared architecture.

The authored backward bearing is a three-part receiving face, rear seat and
diagonal support. FRAME may select it after MOSAIC confirms an actual receiving
response, reclaiming its own provisional local endings when capacity is needed.
It adds no forward bridge cells. The finite alternate handedness and smaller
fallback remain ordinary grammar candidates, charged to the same allowance.

## Reciprocal proposal protocol

[The coordinator](../../native/experiments/gentek_swarm/reciprocal.cpp) freezes
existing native material, original protected voids, exclusive territories and
the declared finite interface band. It preserves the initial generators'
protected passages as well. Each round observes the previous immutable pair;
each Gentek may replace only its own uncommitted proposal. Canonical FRAME-first
claim arbitration accepts complete compatible proposals or restores the previous
complete proposal. Invocation order does not grant ownership.

The initial pair and up to four revisions are retained, including declined
proposals. Identical consumed solver inputs may reuse a completed earlier result
with its original source-round receipt and zero additional donor-solver debit.
Changed inputs require new work. Digest matches are confirmed by exact
architectural comparison before fixed-point or oscillation decisions.

Material-ID changes, occupied-footprint changes, category changes and provenance
changes are separate evidence. The decisive two-sided geometry metric requires
occupied-footprint changes in both Genteks; material substitution alone cannot
qualify it. A final per-cell ledger records owner, structural instance, rule and
accepted revision history.

## Joints, native materials and honest closure

`FOREIGN_JOINED` requires an original-source-bound connector, compatible exact
facing, an actual peer-owned native receptor, solid structural contact/backing
and a bounded contiguous material path. New assemblies retain their own required
foreign exports. An engineered local ending is recorded independently; it never
discharges an original required foreign connection. Unresolved obligations
remain `STILL_REQUIRED` and prevent Apply.

The frozen MOSAIC material vocabulary includes native Stone13, which is a
**powder**, not a solid. Ordinary generated Stone is preserved exactly. The
experimental MOSAIC receiving rule may replace existing Stone contact/backing
cells with native LimestoneBlock38, a solid, only on its own admitted and
satisfied receptor. The unchanged occupancy/category assignment and each
13-to-38 material receipt are retained. The `native_bearing_off` control isolates
this material variant. No production material definition or physics is changed.

UNSAT, bounded exhaustion, invalid claims, cancellation, unresolved reservations
and final-path failures remain distinct. A successful categorical assignment or
geometric joint does not demonstrate stability after native simulation.

## Resource and scientific contract

The [preregistration](../../native/experiments/gentek_swarm/RECIPROCAL_REGISTRATION.json)
retains 2M FRAME work, 2M MOSAIC work, 4M categorical work and 2M shared work,
16MiB aggregate admitted storage, 64 FRAME instances, 192 MOSAIC instances,
eight total reservations, recursion depth six and at most four revision rounds.
Rejected and repeated work is charged. Live own-part capacity can be reclaimed;
spent work cannot. Cancellation and insufficient capacity never authorize a
partial commit.

Known seeds 605, 1701, 2718, 4099, 8191 and 12289 comprise the established 108
cases. Holdouts 19457 and 32771 require a clean committed policy selection before
opening. Unchanged B/C/E8, hard-only E8, vocabulary, hierarchy, shared ownership,
one-sided revision, one/two/three/four rounds and final-validation diagnostics
remain separate controls. A changed final FRAME also receives a MOSAIC no-offer
control on that exact frame and accepted mask with an explicitly separate
control budget. All failed cases are retained.

Build the focused suites with `make -f Makefile.reciprocal` from the native
experiment directory. `SANITIZE=1` requires a separate build directory. The
compiled definition fingerprint and clean source manifest bind binaries and
evidence. `run_reciprocal.py --qualification` adds frozen-control reproduction;
the offline gallery shows original material arrays and round/ownership overlays.

## Workbench dependency and acceptance

The dependent bridge starts from explicitly recorded accepted main and imports
the frozen native interface. Donors #684/#685 are not implicit merge targets.
Gentek definitions remain authored outside Workbench. The intended single lazy
composition panel provides territory strokes, bounded shared extent, expression,
seed, structural controls, independent decoration/destruction, native preview
and proposal inspection.

Only native `can_apply` authorization may reach the existing authoritative
prepared-World transaction. Exact source generation/revision, expected material
IDs, body/protected masks, whole-patch admission and acknowledged history protect
Apply and Undo. A native standalone test is not Workbench acceptance. Actual
graphical Generate, Apply, Undo, Save, process exit and new-process reopen, plus a
source-matched Windows evaluator, remain explicit independent gates.

## Checkpoint status and documentation map

The [SWARM-004 research record](../../native/experiments/gentek_swarm/SWARM-004.md)
records the complete qualified V1 and V2 known comparisons. V1 has four shared
reciprocal Apply examples using small terminal bearings; larger multipart trusses
remain blocked by original obligations. V2 produces real articulated triangles
in partial outcomes but worsens the registered primary comparison and remains
unselected. These results do not establish the complete large shared-zone
truss/arch acceptance example. A predeclared fixed-territory round interaction
supplement precedes final selection and unopened holdouts. Windows and Workbench
acceptance remain separately source-bound; no predecessor evidence is rewritten.

This contract owns the experimental interfaces and invariants. The linked
preregistration owns experimental budgets and comparison policy; the programme
issue owns scope. Existing native material, World limits, renderer, physics,
Viaduct identities and save schemas are unchanged. The documentation index,
retrieval route, roadmap and handover point here; Workbench transaction details
remain with its separate bridge contract.
