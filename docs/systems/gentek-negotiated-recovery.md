## Prospective connector feasibility

The new option screens a prospective FRAME part before its original foreign
obligation is frozen. It retains the authored native grammar and the SWARM-005
terminal system. The screen considers the source's real structural face,
facing, family, candidate footprint and existing occupancy; protected context;
MOSAIC territory and authorized shared space; finite receiving span; remaining
parts and reservations; and the original MOSAIC tile's receiving predicates.

The bounded hypothetical tile probe creates no material and does not solve a
categorical assignment. Its category mask is an opportunity, not a bid or proof.
Native cap/terminal footprints are checked against actual prospective cells.
Reserved source opportunities cannot be silently consumed by subsequent local
branches, braces or cohesion patches. If a part cannot support its required
connection within the native alternatives, source generation refuses it before
publishing its obligation. These refusals and genuine local terminations are
reported separately from foreign joints.

After the original FRAME plan is frozen, its required foreign connectors remain
immutable. Later revisions cannot delete, downgrade, cap away or fabricate
fulfillment of that ledger. The final validator accounts for every original
required obligation and every newly exported assembly obligation.

## Reciprocal architectural protocol

Each round reads immutable accepted prior proposals. FRAME remains responsible
for FRAME material and authored truss, bracket, member and receiving-bearing
substitutions. MOSAIC remains responsible for its material, parts, categories and
typed receivers. Canonical complete-footprint arbitration is independent of the
order in which the two generators are invoked.

MOSAIC recovery preserves the original solved categorical assignment and all
accepted native masonry. It considers existing native bearings and finite
supported corbels: two-cell-thick authored parts of length two, four or six,
with three permitted orientations and a real original native host pair. The
existing Stone-to-Limestone bearing variant is explicit and receipted. A new
corbel cannot use another new corbel as its host. Host category, exact owning
part footprint, actual material, clearance and authorized territory must agree.
This preserves existing motifs and negative space; it does not replace the
architecture with a generic block.

The sequence is source offer, native receptor bid, provisional reservation,
FRAME geometry revision, native joint proof and acceptance or counteroffer.
Reservations carry exact source connector/slot, host and receiver part IDs,
port ID, candidate, native edits, original and prior material identities and
charged work. A port reserved for one source cannot satisfy a competing source.

An unjoined bid survives one full FRAME reply. A later immutable round may
withdraw only its own unchanged provisional edits and try the next candidate.
Earlier receipts remain in earlier snapshots. Joined geometry and protected
passages are retained. The coordinator creates no decorative bridge or material
path; it arbitrates and proves the Genteks' actual proposals.

## Bounded alternatives and convergence

Candidate order is finite and deterministic: existing bearings, then authored
corbel lengths/orientations along at most two original face endpoints and a
fourteen-cell forward interface. At most sixty-four candidates per source slot
are examined in one round. Failed candidates consume the same aggregate work
allowance and advance a retained cursor. There are at most eight reservations
and four reciprocal rounds.

Repeated geometry is insufficient to stop recovery while a pending reservation
or unexamined alternative remains authorized. Exhaustion of this finite
catalogue does not prove that all possible architecture is impossible. Native
categorical UNSAT, missing receiver/backing, protected/context collision,
competing claim, connector capacity, unconstructible FRAME span, unqualified
material path, candidate exhaustion, round cap, work exhaustion, cancellation
and oscillation remain distinct evidence.

The initial envelope is unchanged: FRAME two million work units, MOSAIC two
million, categorical solver four million and shared coordination two million;
conservative storage sixteen MiB. Proposal and verification costs are both
included. The [native runbook](../../native/experiments/gentek_swarm/SWARM-006.md)
states the versioned metadata accounting precisely. A failure stops before the
next unfunded operation; no unsuccessful debit is refunded.

## Native authority and material proof

Only the native `assemble` invocation can mint an opaque immutable in-process
run authority. It binds the exact input/options/limits, original source,
accepted and rejected proposal snapshots, categorical and search histories,
revisions, native requests and observed resource receipts using streaming
SHA-256. The public composition cannot mint, replace or reseal that authority.
All bounded metadata is admitted before hashing. Clearing the public recovery
switch or transplanting public fields from a different run cannot bypass it.

This is an explicit trust boundary around the executed native generator. It
authenticates what native code produced and charged; source review, stress tests
and deterministic replay must still establish that its instrumentation is
correct. It avoids solving the same original seed and rejected searches twice
during a single authorization. It does not grant material validity by itself.

Independent certificates check every accepted MOSAIC variant against its exact
original host, footprint, material changes, source and typed receptor. The
unchanged authoritative validator then checks all historical ownership and
protection, compiled FRAME grammar, exact compatible endpoints and facing,
solid native backing, continuous bounded material paths and original foreign
obligations. A receiver reservation or local termination cannot become a joint
through a receipt assertion. Only complete closure authorizes atomic Apply.

Saved JSON, preview pixels and public digests cannot mint native authority.
Historical evidence is inspectable; it requires fresh source-matched native
generation and validation to authorize a new application. Failed negotiation
does not write the live World.

## Comparisons and acceptance boundaries

All OFF controls preserve their preceding native semantics. SWARM004 V1/V2 and
SWARM005 V3 remain available; recovery uses explicit `SWARM006 4` and schema 6,
with the new panel control OFF by default. Source feasibility terminations,
foreign qualified joints and still-required obligations have separate counts.

The [comparison runner](../../native/experiments/gentek_swarm/SWARM-006-EXPERIMENTS.md)
retains complete native requests/responses, LE16 material-plane hashes,
process failures, work and memory distributions, rejected proposals, exact
replay/reversal and matched no-offer/frozen-peer observations. The revealed
360-execution SWARM-005 cohort is development evidence. Its primary comparison
found zero newly applicable cases; it is never relabeled as unseen validation.
A new corpus is preregistered only after exact source and search policy freeze,
with every case retained and no post-reveal algorithm tuning.

Synthetic blank native crops reproduce declared controls where available.
They do not reproduce the owner's saved 2048-square World, unknown crop/context
or physical Windows/NVIDIA run. Actual graphical source and pristine Windows
package exercises, native material-plane Apply/Undo/Save/reopen comparison and
owner NVIDIA acceptance are distinct gates. Automated Windows CI is not owner
acceptance. The programme remains HOLD until its recorded gates are satisfied;
Ready, merge and production promotion require explicit owner authority.
