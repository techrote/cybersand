## Actual 12-record bounded result

Every arm of `my-canvas` logged **zero** hard-surface packet executions. The same-source `bench-canvas` recorded **122/142** packet samples for C1/B2 and **143/143** for C4/B3. Real-input parsing and arithmetic were independently performed in the connected GitHub-data execution, with all 12 records accounted for.

| Pair and aggregate | Bench gain (ms) | Zero-packet gain (ms) | Descriptive difference (ms) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| C1→B2 simulation p50 | +0.206383 | +0.208036 | **−0.001653** |
| C4→B3 simulation p50 | +0.086287 | +0.068122 | **+0.018165** |
| C1→B2 simulation mean | +0.723760 | +0.416585 | +0.307175 |
| C4→B3 simulation mean | +0.100184 | +0.122370 | −0.022186 |
| C1→B2 simulation p95 | +2.760954 | +0.771486 | +1.989468 |
| C4→B3 simulation p95 | +0.194995 | +0.116665 | +0.078330 |

The forward **p50 whole-simulation gain on the benchmark (0.206383 ms) was marginally smaller than the zero-packet canvas gain (0.208036 ms)**. Reverse p50 retains a small positive contrast of 0.018165 ms. The p95 contrast differs greatly between comparisons: +1.989468 ms forward versus +0.078330 ms reverse. The experiment therefore **does not isolate a reproducible whole-tick gain attributable to the packet implementation**. It is consistent with timing variation or non-packet changes; it neither proves the packet code has no other effects nor supplies an unbiased adjusted estimate.

Separate B4-006 exposure accounting continues to support the prior source-qualified **packet-stage** reduction. Its measured packet-work saving remains 43.284/50.047 ms across the bench forward/reverse arms. Packet event frequencies are not identical between arms, and absent raw per-tick records the packet and simulation distributions cannot be aligned. Do not subtract stage p50 from simulation p50 or sum p95 figures.
