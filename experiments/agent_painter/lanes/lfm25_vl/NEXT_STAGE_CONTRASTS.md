## Why these three new teacher hypotheses exist

Independent original raw [DEVELOPMENT error audit](DEVELOPMENT_ERROR_AUDIT.md)
found **11/12 complete JSON first-pass generations but 2/12 strict programs**,
and both trained 48/120-step adapters generated the *same wrong T-shaped
three-endpoint* pipe. Cross-family schema interference included Architecture
`openings`/`thickness` inside Greebles panels and missing
`region`/`clearance` in Architecture. More steps over the original six
independent TRAIN structures are not justified.

`contrastive_proposals_v1.json` deliberately exposes only three **hand-authored,
unadmitted** compiler-v2 teacher hypotheses:

- **Greebles G-C1:** six separated, strictly two-dimensional panel cassettes
  in two banks with an empty central service chase and five actual permitted
  panel details. Panels use the exact `border`, `fasteners`, `density`,
  `box`, `detail`, `kind` fields — not room/opening fields.
- **Pipework P-C2:** a connected *dual-cross* tree with eight nodes, two
  independent degree-four junctions, six graph endpoints, two upper risers,
  two lower risers, exactly seven orthogonal edges, `diameter=3`,
  `fittings=true`, `cycles=0`. This is **not** another single-tee or a
  new paraphrase/seed of the original three-riser held-out example.
- **Architecture A-C3:** flat orthographic maintenance hall with two
  explicitly permitted opposing wall openings, two horizontal raised
  platforms, two vertical supports, one horizontal floor, one vertical wall,
  external stairs and a five-cell-clear horizontal route. This establishes
  geometric clearance only, not gravity/jump/climb playability.

These are merely hypothesis examples for shepherd/reviewer selection; they
do **not** prove global novelty against every peer lane or suitability as
training data. The pipeline never writes common TRAIN, never opens FINAL
targets/images, performs no model inference and does not modify Art authority.
