## Interpretation

- **GREYHOUND:** actual computation removal works immediately. Eleven source layers skipped produced nearly 2× lower decision latency while retaining valid typed output, but pushed the worm into an extreme BRANCH attractor.
- **SCRAMBLED EGGS:** arbitrary middle-layer reordering does not yield useful randomness in this specimen; it produces a stable terminal personality: quit immediately.
- **BRANCHWITCH:** the deliberately intended personality graft worked. It retained a functioning, nonterminal worm and strongly biased it toward branching while still being somewhat faster than stock.
- The BRANCH attractor also appeared in the earlier Rusty Spoon middle-FFN scoop, suggesting that both weight attenuation and structural middle-layer surgery can expose a similar action preference. This is a useful follow-on clue, not a causal proof.

These are single-seed W0 probes, not native artwork or generalisation. The stock p50 differs across separate hosted runners, so speed claims use **within-job paired stock/mutant ratios**, not cross-run absolute timings.

## Phase 2 — SKELEHOUND, AEROHOUND, and BRANCHWITCH buoyancy

### Final working 8-layer hounds

The first pure 8-layer concepts exposed a real decision-graph input constraint: a pure-convolution SKELEHOUND and full-attention-only AEROHOUND left required no-cache attention inputs unconnected and aborted in ggml before serving decisions. Those failures are preserved. The final variants retain only enough attention “ballast” to keep both full/sliding input paths live while remaining **8/24-layer** wrecks.
