# GENTEK-SWARM-005 — Native source feasibility and supported receiver pilot

**Status:** Experimental / Draft / HOLD. Not part of frozen GENTEK-SWARM-004 results. No authorization to merge or promote. This pilot does not establish real-world physical load-bearing stability.

## Motivation / owner discoveries

The owner found that native shared-territory previews can look like cooperative architecture but remain **blocked** by unsatisfied foreign-required connections. Owner Windows reproduction: seed 605, 2 rounds, interface reach 13, FRAME left / MOSAIC right. Viaduct-like 192×112 and Irregular Megastructure 192×112 both blocked when sharing was enabled (5,770 and 6,010 material cells, 0 joint witnesses). Irregular 128×128 blocked with 4,889 material cells, 1 joint witness, `round_cap`. These remain diagnostic regressions and must **not** be relabelled successful.

Initial source study found two distinct problems:

1. Some FRAME required outputs face immediately into a disallowed fixed/native world edge. They have neither a legal local native termination nor a reachable MOSAIC territory. No number of negotiation rounds can create a receiver there.
2. Some admitted MOSAIC motifs expose valid native, two-cell supported material contact but no registered exact opposite-facing receptor. Existing architecture may offer an authentic connection without rerasterizing or fabricating geometry.

## Isolated, opt-in native policy

Two independent defaults-off options in `assembly004::Options`:

- `compact_native_termination`: before the original FRAME plan freezes, a charged source-time candidate preflight rejects new mandatory output sockets which lack even two native cap cells and cannot face reachable peer territory. Where its ordinary termination fails, FRAME may try a finite genuine steel compact weld with an input socket, a new part, a recorded joining counterpart and nonzero native material. Only a fully authored and installed local joint can satisfy the uncommitted FRAME source. No existing original foreign-required contract is removed, downgraded or capped after publication.
- `supported_native_receptors`: a later MOSAIC revision may author one typed receiving offer per **predeclared hard FRAME slot**, only where its accepted native motif has adjacent solid structural contact and backing cells belonging to the same owned instance, a visible source connector points at it, and the intervening path is empty, eligible, within the finite interface envelope and unprotected. Each new port has a source slot/connector tag and a full evidence receipt. The receiver **never** counts as a joint by itself. FRAME must still build its own path and the unchanged `validate_composition` must certify `FOREIGN_JOINED` and every other required obligation before `can_apply`.

Both options are explicitly billed to the finite source/generator/shared work budgets. Each tagged receiver in **every historical accepted round** is independently rebound to the preceding FRAME slot, original required connector, owning MOSAIC part, exact material cells, and receipt. An invalid or forged tag/owner/backing/face/root fails closed. No applied World changes are made from a blocked or exhausted result.

- Focused 12-case paired native comparison: `protected_corridor` and `narrow_neck`, profiles `sparse_regular`, `dense_cohesive`, `branching_irregular`, seeds 605 and 19457; both new options on versus both off. **Three originally blocked cases became complete/Apply-authorized**: `narrow_neck/sparse_regular/605`, `narrow_neck/dense_cohesive/19457`, `narrow_neck/branching_irregular/605`. Other cases remain negative. This is selected development evidence, **not** a rate estimate over unseen worlds.

This is a necessary and insufficient partial improvement. Its source-time complementary option was developed afterwards.
- `protected_corridor/sparse_regular/605` remains blocked but gains a genuine foreign-joined obligation and displays the other two original still-required obligations. Earlier complete claims based on raw material or socket counts would be invalid.
- A new `swarm005_research_tests.cpp` exercises two qualified rescues, a partial-negative witness, source controls, determinism, historical receiver binding mutations and atomic unfunded refusal. Existing SWARM-004 native suites must continue passing without selecting either opt-in flag.
