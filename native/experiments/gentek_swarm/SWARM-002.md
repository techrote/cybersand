## Research conclusion

**The narrow architectural mechanism works. It is not yet a generally better
boundary strategy.** Real FRAMEWEAVER reservations change MOSAIC's category
domains before `PreparedRegions::compile`, and the fixed-seed no-offer ablation
demonstrates different selected native motifs. The island and foreign-solid
examples show useful receiving masonry around recognisable mechanical frames.

The complete primary comparison is six unchanged hostile fixtures, three intact
profiles and two seeds: **36 matched cases**. The default policy keeps required
mechanical sockets hard and treats optional contacts as preferences. A separate
all-hard experiment repeats the same 36 cases. No seventh fixture was needed.

| Fact | Default policy, 36 cases | All-hard, 36 cases |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| MOSAIC complete | 20 | 10 |
| Proven UNSAT | 12 | 26 |
| Work exhausted, satisfiability unknown | 4 | 0 |
| Offered / geometrically admitted slots | 179 / 141 | 179 / 141 |
| Selected compatible responses | 73 | 39 |
| Validated material joints | 72 | 38 |
| Affected tiles selecting a different category from the no-offer ablation | 28 | 17 |
| Unresolved mandatory frame connectors after final validation | 70 | 75 |

The SWARM-001 control solves MOSAIC in **26 of 36** cases, with ten retained
UNSAT outcomes and 73 unresolved mandatory frame connectors in aggregate.
The default candidate therefore introduces six additional failed solves. Its
reduction in late seam construction must be considered alongside that cost.

For the **20 matched cases where both methods solve**, post-hoc additions fall
from **654 to 262 cells (59.9%)**, while validated contacts rise from **45 to 72**.
Shared bridge cells alone fall from **414 to 262 (36.7%)**. Mandatory unresolved
connectors change from 34 to 33, not to zero. These are geometric facts, not a
quality score, stability proof or human aesthetic acceptance.

The candidate also adds **548 preplanned FRAMEWEAVER terminal cells** in those
20 cases (927 across all 36). They remain separately visible and counted. The
post-hoc reduction is a property of the combined reserve-first experiment; it
cannot all be credited to MOSAIC. The no-offer control establishes categorical
causality, not an allocation of the entire B-to-C improvement between authors.

## Protocol and ownership

The experiment has one directed, finite preparation sequence:

1. Reuse the fixed disjoint territories, immutable context and protected voids.
2. FRAMEWEAVER grows its existing mechanical vocabulary and defers peer-facing
   terminal decisions. Reinforcement and nonstructural cohesion finish before
   the protected structural core snapshot.
3. One authored, transition-local terminal pass reserves real point or segment
   contacts. The final frame material and at most eight reservations freeze.
4. MOSAIC consumes those reservations while constructing its domains. Its own
   receiving termination or buttress categories compete under unchanged
   GEN-003 cardinal adjacency and bounded propagation/backtracking.
5. MOSAIC rasterizes only a complete solved assignment. The two Genteks' material
   plans are retained before any shared join exists.
6. Shared validation may add one minimal path per reservation, at most 16 path
   cells including endpoints. Exact exit/entry directions, ownership, immutable
   cells, protected geometry and the original reservation envelope are checked.
7. An optional residual-only RECEPTOR pass follows only a qualified interface.

There is no simultaneous global optimizer, re-entrant negotiation loop or new
production IR. No post-solve FRAMEWEAVER or MOSAIC adaptation pass is needed by
this candidate. Their contribution counters are explicitly zero. The predecessor
adapters remain available unchanged in control B.

## Reservations and termination

`TransitionSlot` in `negotiated.hpp` contains stable geometry-derived identity,
structural owner/connector, point or short bearing segment, cardinal facing,
required mechanical obligation, hard versus optional preference, finite usable
envelope, protected clearance and an explanatory reason. FRAMEWEAVER never
receives or emits a MOSAIC category ID and never aligns itself to MOSAIC's tile
pitch.

The qualified vocabulary is deliberately only **point sockets and bearing
segments**. `PassageOpening` is reserved in the research enum but explicitly
rejected as unsupported; this programme does not claim negotiated traversal
openings. Ordinary MOSAIC portal motifs still exist, and a bearing can cause a
portal to be replaced by a closed termination. A material bearing is not a door.

The terminal alternatives are a short receiving member with a transverse end
plate, the original narrow cap, or a flush terminal plate. Whole footprints must
fit. A successful local cap has an actual required-input counterpart. Where no
piece fits, the connector stays required/unfulfilled; relabeling it cannot
discharge the obligation. The final mesher resolves a required connector only
through its exact ID, instance, geometry, facing and a recorded material path.

Positive strength values are inspectable activation metadata in this version,
**not native GEN-003 weights or a ranking guarantee**. Zero disables an optional
preference. Required mechanical slots remain hard under the default policy;
the all-hard control additionally hardens optional slots.

Reserve-first termination is not uniformly better. In the development matched
frame-only sweep, stepped/dense/605 reduced unresolved mandatory ends from six
to three and island/sparse from four to two. Island/branching/605 and
concave/branching/1701 retained worse intermediate frame-only counts. The final
cross-owner joins have their own outcomes and do not erase these decisions.

## MOSAIC domain decisions

The negotiated entry point is `negotiate_mosaic`, which calls an optional branch
of the existing motif generator. `impose_reservations` runs **before** the actual
unchanged `PreparedRegions::compile` call. MOSAIC searches the offered envelope
for an eligible outward face, then evaluates its own authored footprint
predicates to admit a receiving closed termination or buttress. These are real
finite category-domain changes, not raster patches.

The category count remains **16**, the logical grid stays within **16×16**, and
cardinal doorway/arch adjacency is unchanged. A partial-tile buttress has its own
authored stepped receiving-pier geometry. Its footprint depends on the tile's
admitted spans, not a foreign endpoint, bridge path or bespoke per-slot shape.
It is not an arbitrary rotated part assembler.

Every tile retains original, hard-only, preferred and effective final domains;
influencing slot IDs and compatible categories; selected category; and fallback
and preference receipts. Every response retains its selected contact/facing and
checks that material actually exists there after rasterization. Every solved
category is retained for the same-frame no-offer comparison.

Soft semantics are explicit: one preferred-domain attempt receives half the
declared solver allowance, and a **proven UNSAT** may use the remaining half for
one hard-only fallback. No fallback follows exhaustion. All-hard or unchanged
domains use one solve. An empty hard intersection or a hard slot with no receiving
geometry is protocol UNSAT without calling GEN-003; unsupported or malformed
slots are Invalid. Ordinary solver UNSAT and work exhaustion remain distinct.

GEN-003 exposes successful preparation work, but not failed-attempt used work or
backtrack counts. Raw attempts use `null` when work is unavailable, with an
explicit configured ceiling. Backtracks remain unavailable. No partial
assignment is rasterized after a failed categorical solve.

## Controls and findings

Every case retains four stages from the same source, fixture, seed, profile and
material catalogue:

| Stage | Geometry |
| --- | --- |
| A | Independent full-canvas generation, then ownership clipping |
| B | Exact SWARM-001 territory-aware generation, post-freeze adapters and typed shared joints |
| C | FRAMEWEAVER reservations, pre-solve MOSAIC response and bounded final validation |
| D | The identical C structural result, plus separately identifiable optional residual morphology |

An additional **same-frame/no-offers** ablation holds candidate frame material,
protected masks, seed, native vocabulary and solver limits constant while
removing only reservations. C's pre-join image separates native architectural
selection from the final shared material path.

Default policy, considering only cases where both B and C solve:

| Fixture | Cases | B / C post-hoc cells | B / C validated contacts | Affected tiles with counterfactual category changes, full fixture group |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Stepped / diagonal | 2 | 127 / 16 | 9 / 5 | 2 |
| Concave intrusion | 0 | — | — | 0 |
| Protected corridor | 6 | 179 / 29 | 17 / 10 | 5 |
| Foreign solid | 4 | 124 / 118 | 6 / 19 | 7 |
| Island | 4 | 148 / 20 | 12 / 26 | 11 |
| Narrow neck | 4 | 76 / 79 | 1 / 12 | 3 |

The protected-corridor reduction accompanies fewer contacts and is not by itself
an architectural win. Narrow-neck joins increase, but post-hoc cells also rise.
The concave group has no successful candidate; its zeros cannot be counted as
efficient seams.

Two representative matched architectural examples:

- **Island / branching / 605:** two control contacts and 25 post-hoc cells become
  six contacts and four cells. Three affected tiles change category under the
  ablation, with 20 separately counted preplanned terminal cells. The candidate
  resolves every mandatory frame connector; the control retains one. The gallery
  shows a frame received into a masonry rim instead of a thin late loop.
- **Foreign solid / branching / 605:** two control contacts and 27 post-hoc cells
  become five contacts and 23 cells. Two affected categories change and 14
  preplanned terminal cells are added. Both retain five unresolved mandatory
  connectors. Its stepped receiving masonry is a local positive example, not a
  fully valid whole-frame result; RECEPTOR is withheld.

Across the default corpus, 15 cases contain an affected selected-category change;
20 responses become categorically/geometrically compatible where the no-offer
result was not. Many other compatible motifs would have appeared anyway. The
gallery and raw decision tables make that distinction inspectable.

### Retained failures

The six concave cases yield two UNSAT and four work-exhausted outcomes under the
default policy. The work exhaustion is in GEN-003's constrained preferred solve,
not envelope scanning, and the same-frame no-offer solve succeeds in the relevant
cases. Increasing the budget or treating exhaustion as proof of impossibility
would hide the experiment's cost.

Making every offer hard produces **26 UNSAT cases**, including geometrically
inadmissible optional offers. This is a negative result for indiscriminate hard
negotiation. The primary hard-required/soft-optional policy is better, but still
less reliable than the predecessor. Failed, exhausted and unresolved cases stay
in the default gallery and raw corpus.

## Residual finishing

RECEPTOR's implementation is unchanged. The wrapper invokes its existing
transition adaptation against the final C material as immutable context, with
eligibility restricted to the envelopes of **already joined** reservations.
Protected passages, occupied cells, unresolved envelopes and foreign cores stay
excluded. Residual catchments belong to one frozen material component, with
separation between different components; newly added chains cannot manufacture
a connection between previously disconnected structures.

Residual instances are explicitly nonstructural, have no connectors or hard
offers, and never alter C's decisions or footprints. The pass is withheld unless
MOSAIC completed, the interface has a qualified joint, and every mandatory frame
connector and required reservation is resolved. The default corpus enables it
in only three cases, adding 415 labelled cells. This demonstrates a bounded
optional finishing mechanism, **not that it improves architectural quality**.

## Bounds and qualification

The existing request ceilings remain in force: at most 256×128 material cells,
64 default frame instances, 192 MOSAIC motif instances, finite candidate/frontier
caps, 2,000,000 charged generator work per request, 4,000,000 total categorical
work allowance and unchanged 256 KiB GEN-003 scratch. The frame plan admits its
additional snapshot/slot memory before planning.

New protocol bounds are eight slots, at most 32-cell bearing segments, envelopes
no larger than 64×64, and at most eight final joints with 16 path cells including
endpoints. No global solver or unbounded repair loop exists. Fixed-array
validation, component labeling, hashing and evidence serialization are separately
bounded by the finite canvas; they are not mislabeled as measured candidate work
or peak allocator telemetry.

`run_negotiated.py` builds in a fresh fingerprinted directory, requires a clean
checkout for qualification, fingerprints every research source/header/tool and
the unchanged donor inputs, and verifies custody again after the run. It also
extracts and rebuilds the exact predecessor from Git. The full **171-case legacy
corpus** must match every behavioral field; only source-dependent identity fields
and the compiled definition hash suffix are normalized. Material digests, actual
arrays, parts, connectors, boundaries, failures, solver work and metrics remain
exact.

The focused successor suite covers the full 72-case matrix, replay/reversed offer
enumeration, no-offer legacy material parity, RECEPTOR independence, hard
incompatibility, solver exhaustion, slot capacity and frame budgets. Both legacy
and successor suites run under ASan/UBSan. LeakSanitizer is disabled on the host;
no leak-check pass is claimed. The retained independent review supplies its own
probes and checks real joined paths, source-control equality and research claims.
Actual final-source command outcomes and exact HEAD/tree identity belong to the
evidence `manifest.json` and PR handoff, not a self-referential commit field here.

Local documentation/repository checks retain the predecessor's six missing
companion-workspace expected-fact references. Historical M11 integrity verifies
18 records and 28 hashes after fetching its exact historical object. Unchanged
retrieval evaluation is hit@1 19/32, hit@5 30/32, MRR 0.7292. No runtime, Godot,
Windows or GPU campaign is authorized or launched by this research.

## Research disposition

Pre-solve categorical cooperation is worth a **smaller follow-up**, not a larger
framework. The evidence favors testing a bounded optional-offer admission policy:
compare one, two and four optional offers chosen by native geometric relevance,
keeping required sockets hard and preserving the same source/masks/seeds and
budget. Retain candidate C and SWARM-001 as controls. That would test whether
most of the island/receiving-pier benefit survives without the concave
backtracking and whole-plan failure cost. It is a recommendation, not work
started automatically here.

The shared layer should continue to own safety, fixed ownership and path
validation, while Genteks own terminal shape. RECEPTOR remains optional; there
is no evidence that its extra morphology is necessary for the structural result.
Human architectural judgment remains an owner gate.

No Workbench integration, production Gentek registration, production schema
change, Viaduct v1/v2 modification, Viaduct v3 work, renderer/material change,
Ready promotion, auto-merge or merge occurs in this programme.
