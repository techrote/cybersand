## Value contracts and authority

`assembly.hpp` defines immutable finite part/assembly specifications, typed
point/segment sockets, quarter-turn transforms, hard foreign versus terminable
obligations, finite recursion and explicit rejection receipts. Every accepted
material cell has one canonical owner, instance, authored rule and revision.
Welded same-owner cells remain one claim with separately accounted overlap.
Final authorization reconstructs every historical assembly from its compiled
authored factory under the existing shared allowance. Exact claim revisions,
internal joints, sockets and the local-to-native part/rule mapping must match.
A component entirely reusing the original host is identified as host reuse,
never counted as a newly owning material part. Host welds require the original
source cell within two Manhattan cells of the assembly root.

`reciprocal_mosaic.hpp` owns MOSAIC's response. It admits its own territory plus
the declared shared mask, subtracts immutable peer material/protected space and
uses unchanged GEN-003 category semantics. FRAME cannot choose masonry categories.
Unknown failed donor work consumes the full attempted allowance.

The coordinator exchanges immutable round snapshots. FRAME's own authored
grammar selects complete steel parts against MOSAIC's real material receivers.
MOSAIC chooses its own domains/categories against FRAME's real source faces.
Neither writes the other's proposal. Simultaneous claims are canonically
arbitrated; rejection restores the previous complete proposal. The coordinator
can validate geometry and paths but creates zero new architecture cells.

Digest equality includes geometry and semantic offers, not round numbers; an
unchanged pair stops early. A repeated earlier nonconsecutive state is an
oscillation and stops deterministically. Cancellation/invalid/exhausted work
never returns a commit-authorized partial output.

The original required FRAME connector ledger is captured before revisions.
`FOREIGN_JOINED` requires exact facing, unique compatible receptor and an actual
native material path. `LOCAL_TERMINATED` is independent. A neat cap does not
remove a foreign obligation. Complete MOSAIC, complete proposal and fully closed
foreign graph are separately reported. No structural stability is certified.

## Registered controls and budgets

`RECIPROCAL_REGISTRATION.json` is the machine-readable preregistration. Known
108 cases remain the development comparison. Holdouts 19457/32771 are not run
until the selected candidate and source are committed. The first hypothesis is
two rounds; one/three/four rounds, no-offer, one-sided, fixed-territory,
flat/vocabulary and validator-disabled diagnostics isolate the effects.
Validator-disabled output is never application-authorized.

All calls share the original 2M FRAME, 2M MOSAIC, 4M categorical, 2M shared
allowances, 16MiB conservative request storage and eight total reservations.
Repeated failed work consumes those budgets. Original FRAME instance limit64
and MOSAIC192 remain. New grammar expansion may use only remaining capacity.
Changing a frame requires same-frame no-offer controls; frozen E8 remains an
additional independent control. Raw failures remain in every campaign.

Memory admission uses `N * (256 + 64 * round_cap + destruction_bytes) +
(64 + 192) * 16384 + 512 KiB`, where `destruction_bytes` is 64 when enabled and
zero otherwise. The total must fit the unchanged 16 MiB ceiling; a larger
request is refused, not silently admitted with a raised limit.

Destruction is a separate native seeded removal after intact negotiation.
It never rewrites accepted round snapshots. Final authorization reproduces
the removal and verifies survivor materials, ownership and part metadata;
new cells and repainting are invalid. Lost required internal joints are
reported independently. Early refusals retain source identity and consumed
work, including failed original FRAME generation.
