## Scientific interpretation boundaries

The sampled free-profile sheets support FRAMEWEAVER as the clearest provisional
direction for future interactive structural authoring: it produces legible
connected skeletal members and visible changes between profiles. This is a
relative architectural judgment about this bounded experiment. It is not proof
of load-bearing validity, traversal quality, attractive finished levels or
production readiness.

The reviewer also inspected the full island C059 and foreign-solid C093
clipping/own-territory/final comparisons, concave C050 attribution and all five pathological images. The
largest visible change from clipping can come from each Gentek's own territory
awareness; the final SWARM-001 meshing pass supplies the separately demonstrated
bounded contact/bridge increment. Do not credit all clipping-to-final improvement
to shared negotiation. SWARM-001's MOSAIC adaptation is a late template pass,
not SWARM-002's pre-solve category-domain influence.

MOSAIC is architecturally legible but its negative boundary outcomes must remain
visible. Failed GEN-003 used-work statistics remain `null`, backtracks remain
unavailable, and UNSAT is not exhaustion. C096/C097 demonstrate generator-work
exhaustion before MOSAIC solves; they do not demonstrate GEN-003 search exhaustion.
The inherited focused native test independently exercises the donor's UNSAT vs
search-exhaustion distinction. C098 is a memory-admission refusal.

RECEPTOR's free-space shell clusters are visibly disconnected. The available
evidence supports further consideration as a bounded seam/environment specialist,
not a claim that it is already a good primary macrostructure generator. Many
validated contacts are optional; a large number of joints is not an aesthetic
or structural score.

Shared sparse/branching/dense profiles co-vary multiple controls. Profile-level
differences do not isolate the causal effect of branching, irregularity, clutter
or cohesion individually. The registered zero/high affinity comparisons and
intact/damaged comparisons do isolate their respective changed controls.

After meshing, a FRAMEWEAVER result can retain its native `blocked` status even
if a foreign seam has resolved the last mandatory connector: `finish_result`
recomputes unresolved counts without rewriting the original generation status.
Conclusions about final mandatory resolution must use actual counts and receipts.
Neither the opposite `complete` status nor a connected component count is a
physical support certificate.
