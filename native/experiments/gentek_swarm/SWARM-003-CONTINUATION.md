preservation of FRAME outputs, required obligations and their fulfillment.

unresolved required FRAME connectors. Under the current frozen reservation and

about this fixture set and these rules, not a proof about every possible frame

in that classification. The sixteen reservations are not claimed to cause the

interactions between new placements, or instance, candidate and work-budget
consumption. Using this piece as ordinary FRAME termination requires a separately
registered bounded grammar intervention that constructs the actual native part
and records its real mechanical counterpart while preserving the original

The two repeated final-path failure cases each have fixed-output portal-face
