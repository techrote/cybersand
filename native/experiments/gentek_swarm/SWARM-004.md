## Implemented mechanism

The [current contract](../../../docs/systems/gentek-reciprocal-assembly.md),
[design](RECIPROCAL_DESIGN.md) and [registration](RECIPROCAL_REGISTRATION.json)
describe typed components, local/world transforms, bounded recursive assemblies,
independent immutable proposals, exclusive ownership plus an optional shared
zone, exact per-cell rule/instance/revision provenance, deterministic arbitration,
atomic rejection and finite reciprocal rounds. FRAMEWEAVER authors mechanical
parts; MOSAIC retains its original native masonry and GEN-003 categorical solver.
The coordinator does not generate architecture.

Final authorization reconstructs compiled assembly certificates, binds original
source sockets, preserves all original protected passages and requires an exact
native Solid receptor/backing/path witness. Local engineered endings never
discharge a foreign-required socket. Unresolved original and newly exported hard
obligations prevent Apply. Seeded destruction is an independently verified native
removal derivative of the retained intact proposal, not a negotiation round.

All experiments retain the registered ceilings: 2M FRAME + 2M MOSAIC + 4M solver
and 2M shared work, 16MiB admitted memory, 64/192 respective instances, eight
reservations, depth six and at most four rounds. Failed or repeated attempts are
charged. Controls have separately labelled work, never free candidate work.

## What the positive examples actually demonstrate

The shared-zone island examples at seeds 4099 and 12289, branching and dense
profiles, are native Complete and Apply-authorized. MOSAIC changes at round one;
FRAME changes at round two. In branching4099, MOSAIC adds 562 and removes 90
occupied cells; FRAME adds 4 and removes 6. There are four exact native joints.

The FRAME changes are two small three-part backward bearings, each span 2/rise 2
and a 3-by-3 bounding box. Their face, diagonal and seat have authored ownership
and typed internal joints. They are not a large new spanning truss. The original
foreign joints in these four cases are already qualified at round one; the
second response changes FRAME's local support geometry without improving hard
closure. Same-frame no-offer controls are retained, including the seed 12289
cases whose final masonry material is identical to the no-offer control despite
one differing categorical assignment.

Under fixed territories, the corpus contains 45 accepted nested trusses, reaching
span 12/rise 6. For example, narrow_neck/branching/1701 has two such trusses, each
owning 12 member, five post and 17 diagonal cells, and two native foreign joints.
One original hard obligation remains unresolved, so the composition is Blocked.
No V1 Complete case contains a nested truss longer than span 2. The largest new
multi-component Complete assembly is a 3-by-7 bracket owning eight new cells;
longer Complete connections are single members. These distinctions are part of
the acceptance result, not hidden by a combined success count.
