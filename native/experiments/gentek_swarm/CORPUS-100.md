## Retained evidence and interpretation

The full archive's `index.html` opens an offline filterable gallery of all 100. Each `native/C100-NNN.json` is the actual native output, retaining requests, material IDs, instances, connectors, affordances, provenance, rejection reasons and solver/result receipts. `replay/` and `sanitized/` retain separate complete byte-identical outputs. `case_records/`, `case-index.json` and `metrics.csv` expose readable per-case facts without substituting a quality score. `registration.json` and `manifest.json` preserve original identities and 306 command/log receipts.

There are650 raw little-endian uint16 material arrays and500 per-case PNGs, plus all-case contact sheets and three compact galleries. All 99 emitted full-size material PNGs reproduce the native material palette and immutable fixture context exactly. C100-098 returns no material array because of memory admission refusal: its raw and binary arrays remain empty, its native digests remain zero, and its PNG clearly says **NO MATERIAL ARRAY / capacity refusal**. It has not been converted into a successful blank canvas. All other blank UNSAT/exhaustion outputs retain their actual full-size native arrays.

Each pair retains independent generation subsequently clipped to territory, own-territory generation before foreign offers, and final SWARM-001 bounded late adaptation/meshing. The inherited visual title “negotiated transition” refers to this SWARM-001 final control; it is **not** SWARM-002 pre-solve negotiation. Full per-owner material/request/semantic digests and the pair semantic digest are retained; a SHA-256 identifies the combined material array. Every claimed join retains its exact endpoint offers and material path. Failed seams remain in the same raw record.

The semantic views and binary layers distinguish native owner architecture, shared typed bridges, local late adapters and cohesion. The accounting identity is:

`total late additions = shared bridge cells + late adapters excluding cohesion + late cohesion cells`

Existing cohesion is recorded separately from late cohesion. A zero-cell adjacent typed contact is a compatibility witness, not a new bridge. Cohesion cannot resolve a mandatory connector. Unavailable solver work and backtracks are null/explicitly unavailable, never invented zeros. A bounded refusal leaves satisfiability unknown; UNSAT means the attempted categorical problem was proven infeasible. Component counts are descriptive four-neighbor material facts, not load-bearing, stability or traversal scores.

## Architectural review and controls

All 100 native outcomes were visually reviewed in the complete contact sheets. All three pair controls were additionally compared for C048, C050, C053, C059, C062, C073, C081 and C093. Each case's review scope, labels and note are explicit in `corpus100-review.json`; other pair cases are not represented as having received that same detailed visual comparison. The independent reviewer additionally spot-viewed free profiles, island controls, concave attribution and every pathological image.

**FRAMEWEAVER is the clearest provisional primary mode.** Its sparse, branching and dense profiles create legible frame skeletons whose differences a human can see and work with. C001 is a complete simple 925-cell frame; C002 and C003 give visibly different branching/bracing but each retains one unresolved mandatory connector. Damage C017 removes 368 cells from C002, takes material components from 1 to 17, and revokes 69 previously fulfilled mandatory receipts: the evidence does not pretend the damaged frame remains mechanically complete. The bare frame aesthetic is useful for authoring structure, but not a finished building or an engineering claim.

**MOSAIC supplies more building-like material organization, with substantial brittleness at constrained boundaries.** C022/C023 create brick-and-floor motifs with 7,434/8,198 occupied cells. Own-territory output follows concave and island contours, while sparse concave C033 and protected C034 are UNSAT. A compact experimental mode could make this understandable only if it clearly reports UNSAT and retains the prior accepted canvas instead of presenting an empty failure as a valid replacement. That interaction has not been implemented or tested here.

**RECEPTOR is not supported as a primary generator by this corpus.** Its free profiles produce separate shells: 7,17 and21 components for sparse/branching/dense at seed 605, with the same counts at1701. Environmental response exists: changing affinity 0→1 in C042/C043 changes 168 material cells and grows occupied output 566→622, but components increase 10→11. This supports further environmental/seam research, not a conclusion that added wrapping makes a better building.

**FRAME×MOSAIC is the most interesting pair to carry forward as evidence.** C048's stepped meeting has six new bridges and one adjacent contact, but four unresolved required connectors; five of its seven joins are optional-only. C059's island outline improves through own-territory awareness; the final two validated bridges add local continuity while one required connector remains. C053 makes three joins around a protected corridor with zero protected cells filled and two remaining obligations. These are promising partial results, not full seam success.

The negative controls matter just as much. C050's concave meeting has two joins and five unresolved required connectors. C062's narrow-neck gap survives despite 14 adapter cells and 4 late cohesion cells, with zero joins. C073 has zero joins or late additions across the protected FRAME×RECEPTOR boundary. C081 achieves ten bridges and fulfills all 45 required connectors yet remains visually awkward with 13 components. C093 adds 382 adapter cells for four adjacent contacts and zero new bridge cells; in this specific visual authoring comparison the leaner clipped control is preferable. Neither that judgment nor the C059 preference is an objective universal ranking.

Do not expose a FRAME affinity slider on the strength of these cases. After the downstream gates pass, the smallest useful proposed bridge is **experimental FRAMEWEAVER with visible seed and the three intact profile choices**, deterministic Regenerate, Preview, Install and Cancel/Stop as needed, in the existing generator workflow. Extra branching/irregularity/cohesion sliders need separately justified bounded sensitivity evidence; this corpus does not justify displaying every internal knob. Use a clear partial/blocked status and final unresolved count without a giant diagnostics panel. Full receipts can stay in evidence/logs.

MOSAIC is a conditional second candidate only if explicit UNSAT/refusal behavior is practical. RECEPTOR should remain evidence-only for now, with a possible later environmental adjunct. SWARM-002 optional offer caps 1/2/4 have not become artistic controls merely because the research exposes them. This is a **Phase A recommendation**, not the final Phase C selection or implemented UI.
